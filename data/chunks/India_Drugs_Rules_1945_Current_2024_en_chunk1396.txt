17.2.6. Any alteration made to a document shall be signed and dated; 
the alteration shall be done in such a way so as to permit the

17.2.7. Records shall be made or completed when any action is taken 
and in such a way that all significant activities concerning the 
manufacture of pharmaceutical products are traceable. 
Records shall be retained for at least one year after the expiry 
date of the finished product.
