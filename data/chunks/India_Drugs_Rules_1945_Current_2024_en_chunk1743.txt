12.2. Shipping- 
12.2.1. Investigational products shall be shipped in accor dance with the 
shipping orders given by the sponsor. 
12.2.2. A shipment is sent to an investigator only after the following 
two-step release procedure: - (i) the release of the product after 
quality control ("technical green light"); and (ii) the 
authorisation to u se the product, given by the sponsor 
("regulatory green light"). Both releases shall be recorded. 
12.2.3. The sponsor shall ensure that the shipment will be received and 
acknowledged by the correct addressee as stated in the protocol. 
12.2.4. A detailed inventory of the shipments made by the manufacturer 
shall be maintained and shall make particular mention of the 
addressee's identification.
