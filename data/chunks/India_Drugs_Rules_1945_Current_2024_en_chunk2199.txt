Both in -vitro and in -vivo studies should be done. In -vitro studies should 
include Ames' Salmonella Assay and Chromosomal Aberrations (CA) in

of 'gaps'. 
Cytotoxic Anticancer Agents: Genotoxicity data are not required before Phase 
I and II trial s. But these studies should be completed before applying for Phase 
III trials.
