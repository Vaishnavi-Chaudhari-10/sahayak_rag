4.5. Lighting- Adequate lighting shall be provided in all areas to facilitate 
cleaning, maintenance and proper operations. 
4.6. Sewage and refuse - Sewage, refuse and other wastes (e.g., solids, 
liquids, or gaseous by - products from manufacturing) in and from

safe, timely and sanitary manner. Containers and pipes for waste 
material shall be clearly identiﬁed.
