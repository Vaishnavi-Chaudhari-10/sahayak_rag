preparation 
Peel the rhizome of Kumud utpala and cut it into pieces. Add 
required quantiy of water along with Lavana and Amlika to the cut 
rhizomes.. Boil it keeping over medium heat, once properly cooked, 
filter into another stainless steel vessel. S prinkle the cooked Kumuda 
Utpala with the powders of Shunthi , Pippali, Maricha, Methika and 
Jeeraka along with the leaves of Kaitarya and Dhanyaka and mix 

thoroughly. Fry the mixture in Ghrita along with Hingu and make 
them fragrant with Karpura. 
Properties Katu (pungent), madhura (sweet in taste), laghu (light for digestion), 
ushna (hot in potency), ruksha (dry) 
Benefits (Pathya) Pittashamaka (Pacifies pitta dosha), Beneficial in Prameha 
(Increased frequency and turbidity of urine), Daha (Burning), 
Kamala ( Jaundice)
