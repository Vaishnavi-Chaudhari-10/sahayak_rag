4. Standards— 
(a) Description—A slightly opalescent liquid containing dead bacteria 
in suspension. 
(b) Identification—It gives specific agglutination when mixed with the 
serum of the animals infected with S. Abortus Equi organisms. 
(c) Sterility Test—Should comply with the test for sterility described in 
the general monograph on 'Diagnostic Antigens'. 
5. Labelling and Storage — Should comply with the requirements of

Antigens'.
