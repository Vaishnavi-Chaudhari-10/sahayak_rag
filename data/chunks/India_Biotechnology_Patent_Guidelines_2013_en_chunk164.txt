08.05 Time limit for establishing international search report 
The Searching Authority establishes the International Search Report and 
written opin ion or, as the case may be, the declaration referred to in 
sub-rule (3) of rule 19B within a period of three months from the date 
of receipt of the search copy by the Searching Authority, or within a 
period of nine months from the date of priority, whichever expires later.
