 – Positive protection grants IP rights over the subject matter of 
traditional medical knowledge. This may help communities to 
prevent others from gaining illegitimate access to traditional 
medical knowledge or using it for commercial gain without 
equitably sharing the benefits. It may also enable active 
exploitation of traditional medical knowledge by the originating 
community itself, for example, to build up its own enterprises 
based on that knowledge. 
 – Defensive protection does not grant IP rights over traditional 
medical knowledge but aims to stop such rights from being 
acquired by third parties. Defensive strategies include the use 
of documented traditional medical knowledge to preclude, 
oppose or invalidate patents on claimed inventions that are 
directly based on such knowledge.
