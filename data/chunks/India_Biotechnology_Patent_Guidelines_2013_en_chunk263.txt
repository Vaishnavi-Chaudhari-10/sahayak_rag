09.07.02 Consequences of grant 
1) On the grant of patent, every patent is allotted a serial number by 
the electronic system. A Certificate of Patent is generated in the 
prescribed format and an entry in the e -register is made 
simultaneously. In the present electronic system, the date of recordal 
of Patent in the Register of Patents is the same as the date of grant 
of Patent by the Controller. 
2) The complete specification as granted is made available to p ublic 
through official website. 
3) The application, specification and other related documents are 
open for public inspection on payment of prescribed fee. 
4) On grant of patent, the patentee is required to pay the accumulated 
fee within 3 months from the date of recordal of patent in the 
Register of Patents, which is now the same as the date of grant of 
patent
