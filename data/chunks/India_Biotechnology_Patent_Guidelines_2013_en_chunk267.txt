Section 47 
09.07.05 Rights of Patentee 
1) In case of a patented product, the patentee shall have the exclusive 
right to prevent third parties from the act of making, using, 
offering for sale, selling or importing for those purposes that 
product in India. 
2) In case of a patented process, the patentee shall have the exclusive 
right to prevent third parties from the act of using that process, and 
from the act of using, offering for sale, selling or importing for 
those purposes the product
