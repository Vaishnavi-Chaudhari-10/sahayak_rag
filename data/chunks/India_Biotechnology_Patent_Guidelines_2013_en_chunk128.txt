 Receiving Office (RO)receives International Application (IA) 
and does the following verifications: 
 Formality Check :Nationality/Residence

the RO 
 The application must be in a language accepted by the RO 
(English/Hindi) 
 If formality criteria are not satisfied, then IA may be referred 
to IB for further processing.
