18.2. General- 
18.2.1. All handling of materials and products, such as receipt and 
cleaning, quarantine, sampling, storage, labelling, dispensing, 
processing, packaging and distribution shall be done in 
accordance with written procedures or instructions and, where 
necessary, recorded. 
18.2.2. Deviation from instructions or procedures shall be avoided as 
far as possible. If deviations occur, they shall be in accord ance 
with an approved procedure. The authorisation of the deviation 
shall be approved in writing by a designated person, with the 
involvement of the QC Department, when appropriate. 
18.2.3. Checks on yields and reconciliation of quantities shall be 
carried out as necessary to ensure that there are no 
discrepancies outside acceptable limits.
