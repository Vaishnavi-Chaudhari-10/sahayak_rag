GOAT POX VACCINE (LIVING CELL CULTURE) 
1. Synonym— Goat Pox Vaccine (living), attenuated goat pox vaccine. 
2. Definition—Goat Pox vaccine is freeze dried preparation, prepared by 
growing attenuated goat pox virus in kid kidney/testicular cell culture. 
3. Preparation.—Primary kidney/testicular cell cultures of disease free 
kid are used. The monolayers infected with the seed virus are incubated at 37°C.
