6. Storage and Expiry date —Freeze dried vaccine shall be expected to 
retain its potency for periods at temperatures as specified below:— 
—15°C to —20°C — Twenty-four months 
2°C to 4°C — Twelve months 
Room temperature—up to one month. 
The liquid vaccine shall be expected to retain its potency for periods and 
temperatures as specified below: 
2 °C to 4 °C — six months 
Room temperature— seven days
