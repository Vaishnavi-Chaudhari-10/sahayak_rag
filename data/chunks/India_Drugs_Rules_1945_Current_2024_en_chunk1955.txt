A pharmacy shall be provided with dispensing bench, the top of which shall 
be covered with washable and impervious materia l like stainless steel, 
laminated or plastic, etc. 
A pharmacy shall be provided with a cupboard with lock and key for the 
storage of poisons and shall be clearly marked with the word "POISON" in red 
letters on a white background. 
Containers of all concentr ated solution shall bear special label or marked 
with the word "TO BE DILUTED".
