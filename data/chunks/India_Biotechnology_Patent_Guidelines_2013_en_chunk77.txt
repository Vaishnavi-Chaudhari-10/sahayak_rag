05.02 Provisional Specification 
a) When the applicant finds that his invention has reached a stage 
where it can be disclosed but has not attained the final stage, he 
may prepare a disclosure of the invention in the form of a written 
description and submit it to Patent Office as a Provisional 
Specification. 
b) A Provisional Specification secures a priority date for the 
application.
