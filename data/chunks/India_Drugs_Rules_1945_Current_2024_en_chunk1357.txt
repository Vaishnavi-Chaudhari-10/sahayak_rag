11.5. Personal hygiene- 
11.5.1. All personnel, prior to and during employment, as appropriate, 
shall undergo health checkups. Personnel conducting visual 
inspections shall also undergo periodic eye checkups. 
11.5.2. All personnel shall be trained in the practices of personal 
hygiene. A high level of personal hygiene shall be observed by 
all those concerned with manufacturing processes. In 
particular, personnel shall be instructed to wash and sanitise 
their hands before entering production areas. Signs to this 
effect shall be posted and instructions are complied with.
