8. Labelling— 
(a) The label on the ampoule or the bottle shall indicate: 
(i) Proper name. 
(ii) Contents in millilitres or doses. 
(iii) Potency, if any. 
(iv) Batch number, 
(v) Expiry date. 
(b) The label on the outside container shall indicate: 
(i) Proper name. 
(ii) Contents in millilitres or doses. 
(iii) Batch number. 
(iv) Date of manufacture. 
(v) Manufacturing licence No.
