Method of 
preparation 
Clean Ghrita kumari leaves and then place them in a stainless steel 
vessel and cook them with Taad patra/wet palm leaves in order to 
remove its bitterness. After filtering the juice, mix it with Lavana 
and fry it in Ghrita until it becomes saffron -colored and thick 
consistency. Then add spices to it, mix well and cut it into pieces, 
then make it fragrant with karpura. 
Properties Katu (pungent), tikta (bitter), laghu (light for digestion), sheeta (cold 
in potency), snigdha (unctuous) 
Benefits (Pathya) Rasayana (Rejuvenator), Ruchikaraka (imparts taste), Balya 
(Strength promotor), Dhatuvarddhaka (promotes tissue growth)
