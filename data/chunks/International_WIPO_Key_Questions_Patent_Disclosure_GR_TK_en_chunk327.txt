Both the National Seed Office and the Intellectual 
Property and Industrial Property Registries shall, as 
a matter of obligation, consult the Technical Office 
of the Commission before granting intellectual 
or industrial property protection to innovations 
involving elements of biodiversity. They shall in 
all cases provide the certificate of origin issued 
by the Technical Office of the Commission and 
the proof of prior consent.
Reasoned opposition by the Technical Office 
shall prevent registration of the patent or 
protection of the innovation.
Cuba Decree-Law No. 290 of November 20, 2011, on Inventions
