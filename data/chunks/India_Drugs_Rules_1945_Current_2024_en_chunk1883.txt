11.6. Reserve or retention samples- 
11.6.1. The packaging and holding of reserve samples is for the 
purpose of potential future evaluation of the quality of batches 
of API and not for future stability testing. 
11.6.2. Appropriately identiﬁed reserve samples of each batch of API 
shall be retained for one year after the expiry date assigned by 
the manufacturer to the batch, or for three years after 
distribution of the batch, whichever is longer. For APIs with 
retest dates, simil ar reserve samples shall be retained for three 
years after the batch has been completely distributed by the 
manufacturer. 
11.6.3. The reserve sample shall be stored in the same packaging 
system in which the API is stored or in one that is equivalent to 
or more pro tective than the marketed packaging system.
