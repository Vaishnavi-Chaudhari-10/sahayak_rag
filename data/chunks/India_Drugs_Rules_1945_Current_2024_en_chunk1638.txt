15.10. The facility layout shall ensure a unidirectional and segregated flow

16. Complaints:- 
16.1. The person responsible for handling complaints and deciding on the 
measures to be taken to deal with them shall have appropriate training 
or experience in the specific features of the quality control of 
biological products. 
16.2. There are basically two types of complaints, product quality 
complaints and adverse reactions or events.
