Signature 
1. Any notice of withdrawal referred to in Rules 90bis.1 to 90bis.4 
shall be signed by the applicant or, if there are two or more 
applicants, by all of them. An applicant who is considered to be 
the common representative under Rule 90.2(b) shall, not be 
entitled to sign such a notice on behalf of the other applicants.
