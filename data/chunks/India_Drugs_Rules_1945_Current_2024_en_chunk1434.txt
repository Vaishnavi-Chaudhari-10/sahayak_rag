18.5.10.Any significant or unusual discrepancy observed during 
reconciliation of the amount of bulk product and printed 
packaging materials and the number of units produced shall be 
investigated, satisfactorily accounted for and recorded before 
release. 
18.5.11.Upon completion of a packaging operation, any unused batch-
coded packaging materials shall be destroyed and the 
destruction shall be recorded. A documented procedure 
requiring checks to be performed before returning unused 
materials shall be followed, if uncoded printed materials are 
returned back to the stock.
