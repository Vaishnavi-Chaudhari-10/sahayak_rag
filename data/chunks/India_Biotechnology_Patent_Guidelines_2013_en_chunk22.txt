 "Natural person" means any individual or a group of individuals. 
 "Other than natural person" includes a registered company, small 
entity, startup, research organization, educational institute or 
Government. 
 "Legal representative " means a person who in law represents the 
estate of a deceased person. In such a case, the Legal 
Representative is required to file appropriate legal instruments as 
proof of right.
