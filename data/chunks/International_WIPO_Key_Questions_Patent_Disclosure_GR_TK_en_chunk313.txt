Article 26 (Newly added)
The genetic resources referred to in the Patent 
Law means any material taken from human, 
animal, plant or microorganism, containing 
genetically functioning units with actual or 
potential value; the invention-creation accom-
plished depending on the genetic resources 
means those invention-creation of which the 
accomplishment uses the genetic function of 
genetic resources.
