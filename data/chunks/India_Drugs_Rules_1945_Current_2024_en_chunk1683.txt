15. Materials:- 
15.1. All incoming plant materials shall be quarantined and stored under 
appropriate conditions that take into account the degradability of plant 
materials and plant preparations. 
15.2. Only permitted substances shall be used for fumigation and allowable 
limits for their residues together with specifications for the apparatus 
used shall be set.

impurity. The reference standard shall be of a quality appropriate to its
