13.3. Storage areas- 
13.3.1. Storage areas shall be well organised and tidy. Special 
attention shall be paid to cleanliness and good maintenance. 
Any accidental spillage shall be cleaned up immediately using 
methods that minimise the risk of cross - contamination of 
other materials and shall be reported. 
13.3.2. The set -up of storage areas depends on the type of materials 
stored. The areas shall be well labelled and materials stored in 
such a way so as to avoid any risk of cross -contamination. An 
area shall be identified for the quarantine of all incoming plant 
materials. 
13.3.3. Storage areas shall be laid out to permit effective and orderly 
segregation of the various categories of materials stored and to
