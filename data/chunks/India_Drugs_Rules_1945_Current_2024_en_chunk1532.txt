9.5. All equipment such as sterilisers, air-handling and filtration systems, air 
vent and gas filters, water treatment, generation, storage and 
distribution sys tems shall be subject to validation and planned 
maintenance; their return to use shall be approved. 
9.6. Water-treatment plants and distribution systems shall be designed, 
constructed and maintained so as to ensure a reliable source of water of 
an appropriate q uality. They shall not be operated beyond their 
designed capacity. Consideration shall be given to include a testing 
programme in the maintenance of a water system. Water for injection 
shall be produced, stored and distributed in a manner which prevents 
the growth of microorganisms, e.g., by constant circulation at a 
temperature above 70 °C or not more than 4 °C.
