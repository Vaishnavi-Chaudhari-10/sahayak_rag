3. Preparation—Anti-serum is prepared from buffalo-bulls after they have 
been subjected to an injection of killed cultures of virulent strain of Pasteurella 
Septica (Avian) followed by injections of living cultures of the same organism. 
4. Standard—It complies with the requirements described in the general 
provision for anti -sera under Desc ription, Acidity or Alkalinity, Abnormal 
toxicity, Sterility, Solids, Labelling, Storage and Expiry Date. 
Identification—It protects susceptible fowls against infection with 
Pasteurella Septica (Avian) and its homologous strains. 
ANTI-HAEMORRHAGIC SEPTICAEMIA SERUM 
1. Synonym—Pasteurella Septica Antiserum. 
2. Definition—Anti-Haemorrhagic Septicaemia Serum is the serum of 
animals containing the substances that confer a specific protection to susceptible 
animals against virulent strains of Pasteurella Septica.
