Important IP rights may be strengthened or lost when traditional 
knowledge is documented. 
Documenting traditional medical knowledge may be useful for 
the defensive protection of traditional medicine, for example, 
by providing information for prior art searches to preclude 
illegitimate patents (see above under “Defensive Protection”). 
However, documentation does not ensure legal protection for 
the underlying traditional knowledge; it does not prevent this 
knowledge from being used by third parties. In some cases it 
can destroy rights and options if it is undertaken without an IP
