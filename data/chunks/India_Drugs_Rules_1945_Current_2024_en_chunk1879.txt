11.4. Stability monitoring of APIs- 
11.4.1. A documented, on -going testing programme shall be designed 
to monitor the stability characteristics of APIs and the results 
shall be used to conﬁrm appropriate storage conditions and 
retest or expiry dates. 
11.4.2. The test procedures used in stability testing shall be validated 
and be stability-indicating.
