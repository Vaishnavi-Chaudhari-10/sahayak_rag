The parameters to be monitored and recorded in long -term toxicity studies 
should include behavioural, physiological, bioc hemical and microscopic 
observations. In case of parenteral drug administration, the sites of injection 
should be subjected to gross and microscopic examination. Initial and final 
electrocardiogram and fundus examination should be carried out in the non -
rodent species. 
In the case of cytotoxic anticancer agents dosing and study design should be 
in accordance with the proposed clinical schedule in terms of days of exposure 
and number of cycles Two rodent species may be tested for initiating Phase I 
trials. A non-rodent species should be added if the drug has a novel mechanism 
of action, or if permission for Phase II, III or marketing is being sought.
