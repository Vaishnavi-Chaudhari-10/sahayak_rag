(b) Pharmacokinetics, i.e., characterization of drug's absorption,

(c) Pharmacodynamics: Depending on the drug and the endpoints 
studied, pharmacodynamic studies and studies relatin g to drug 
blood levels (pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic studies) may 
be conducted in healthy volunteer subjects or in patients with 
the target disease. If there are appropriate validated indicators 
of activity and potential efficacy, pharmacodynamic data 
obtained from patients may guide the dosage and dose regimen 
to be applied in later studies.
