21. Application for a patent.

(8)
The description shall contain a clear identi-
fication of the origin of genetic or biological 
resources collected in the territory of Uganda 
and that were directly or indirectly used in the 
making of the claimed invention as well as of 
any element of traditional knowledge associ-
ated or not with those resources and that was 
directly or indirectly used in the making of the 
claimed invention without the prior informed 
consent of its individual or collective creators.
