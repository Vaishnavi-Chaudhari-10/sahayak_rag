(15) The Adjudicating Officer shall complete the adjudication of every matter within ninety days from the date fixed 
under sub-rule (7), which is extendable upto a further period of ninety days if sufficient cause exists. 
(16) If the subject -matter of a complaint received under this rule is already in question before the National Green 
Tribunal or any other Court of competent jurisdiction on the date of receipt of the matter, the proceedings under this 
rule shall not be initiated unless such latter case is finally disposed of and the Adjudicating Officer shall make an order 
of adjournment of the proceedings under this sub-rule.
