6. Sanitation:- 
6.1. There shall be written procedures for the sanitation of the MDI 
manufacturing facility. Special care shall be taken to handle residues and 
rinses of propellants. 
6.2. Use of water for cleaning shall be rest ricted and controlled. Routinely 
used disinfectants are suitable for sanitising the different areas. Records 
of sanitation shall be maintained. 

7. Equipment:- 
7.1. Manufacturing equipment shall be of closed system. The vessels and 
supply lines shall be of stainless steel. 
7.2. Suitable check weights, spray testing machines and labelling machines 
shall be provided in the department. 
7.3. All the equipment shall be suitably calibrated and their performance 
validated on receipt and thereafter periodically.
