STREPTOCOCCUS EQUI VACCINE 
1. Synonym—Strangles Vaccine 
2. Definition—Streptococcus equi Vaccine is a phenolised culture of a 
number of different isolates of Streptococcus equi in glucose serum broth. 
3. Preparation—Equal proportions of forty -eight hours old pure cultures of 
different isolates of Str. equi in ser um glucose broth are mixed together. The 
suspension is centrifuged and the deposit is washed with normal saline solution 
after removing the supernatant. The washed cells are suspended in normal saline 
and heated in a water bath at 65°C for two hours. Pheno l and normal saline are 
added to give a final concentration of 1,200 million Str. equi organisms per mo. 
and 0.5 per cent of phenol in the vaccine.
