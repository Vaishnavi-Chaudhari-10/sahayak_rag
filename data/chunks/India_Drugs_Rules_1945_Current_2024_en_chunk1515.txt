6.11.3. Isolator technology- 
6.11.3.1. The use of isolator technology to minimise human 
interventions in processing areas may result in a significant 
decrease in the risk of microbial contamination of 
aseptically manufactured products from the envi ronment. 
There are many possible designs of isolators and transfer 
devices. The isolator and the background environment shall 
be designed so that the required air quality for each zone can 
be realised. Isolators are constructed of various materials 
more or less prone to puncture and leakage. Transfer devices 
may vary from single -door to double -door designs to fully -
sealed systems incorporating sterilisation mechanisms.
