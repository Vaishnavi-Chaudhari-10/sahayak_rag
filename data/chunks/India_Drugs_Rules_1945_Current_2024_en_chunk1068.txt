Dog Safety test —Each of the two susceptible pups aged 8 -14 
weeks shall be injected with vaccine equivalent of 10 vaccinating 
doses from the batch reconstituted with sterile diluent and 
administered in the manner recommended on the label and 
observed for 21 days. None of the pups shall show any 
unfavourable reaction during the period of observation.
