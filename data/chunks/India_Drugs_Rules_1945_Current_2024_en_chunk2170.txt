For most compounds, it is expected that single dose tissue distribution studies 
with sufficient sensitivity and specificity will provide an adequate assessment of 
tissue distribution and the potential for accumulation. Thus, repeated dose tissue 
distribution studies should not be required uniformly for all compounds and 
should only be conducted when appropriate da ta cannot be derived from other 
sources. Repeated dose studies may be appropriate under certain circumstances 
based on the data from single dose tissue distribution studies, toxicity and 
toxicokinetic studies. The studies may be most appropriate for compou nds 
which have an apparently long half life, incomplete elimination or unanticipated 
organ toxicity.
