1.7. The product quality system shall be defined and documented. A quality

2. Quality Risk Management (QRM): 
2.1. Quality Risk Management is a systematic process for the assessment, 
control, communication and review of risks to the quality of the 
medicinal product. It can be applied both proactively and 
retrospectively.
