05.03.02 National phase applications 
In case of national phase applications, the title, description, drawings, 
abstract and claims filed with the international application are taken as 
a complete specification. An application corresponding to an 
International application filed under PCT may be made in Form-1. 
However, the applicant, while filing such application corres ponding to 
an international application designating India, may delete a claim, in 
accordance with the provisions contained in rule 14.
