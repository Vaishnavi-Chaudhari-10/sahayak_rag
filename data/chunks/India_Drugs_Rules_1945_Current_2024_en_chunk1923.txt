18.2. Quality- 
18.2.1. Appropriate GMP concepts shall be applied in the production 
of APIs for use in clinical trials with a suitable mechanism for 
the approval of each batch. 
18.2.2. A quality unit independent from production shall be established 
for the approval or rejection of each batch of API for use in 
clinical trials.
