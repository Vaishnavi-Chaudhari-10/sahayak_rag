13.2.1. Reference samples of biological starting materials shall be 
retained under the recommended storage conditions for at least 
one year beyond the expiry date of the corresponding finished 
product. Reference sampl es of other starting materials 
(other than solvents, gases and water) as well as 
intermediates for which critical parameters cannot be tested in 
the final product shall be retained for at least two years after 
the release of the product if their s tability allows for this 
storage period. Certain starting materials such as components 
of growth media need not necessarily be retained. 
13.2.2. Retention samples of a finished product shall be stored in their 
final packaging at the recommended storage conditions for at 
least one year after the expiry date.
