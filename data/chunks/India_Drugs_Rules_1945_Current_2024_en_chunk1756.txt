1.28. Maximum and minimum room temperatures and relative humidity shall 
be appropriate. Alert and action limits on temperatures and humidity 
shall be set, as appropriate. 
1.29. The operating band or tolerance between the ac ceptable minimum and 
maximum temperatures shall not be made too close. Tight control 
tolerances may be difficult to achieve and can also add unnecessary 
installation and running costs. 
1.30. Cubicles or suites, in which products requiring low relative humidity a re 
processed, shall have well sealed walls and ceilings and shall also be 
separated from adjacent areas with higher relative humidity by means of 
suitable airlocks. 
1.31. Precautions shall be taken to prevent moisture migration that increases 
the load on the HVAC system. 
1.32. Humidity control shall be achieved by removing moisture from the air, or 
adding moisture to the air, as relevant.
