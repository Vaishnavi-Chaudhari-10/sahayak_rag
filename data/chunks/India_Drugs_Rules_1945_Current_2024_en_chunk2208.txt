Note.—Each dose group and concurrent control group not intended to be 
sacrificed early should contain at least 50 animals of each sex. A high dose 
satellite group for evaluation of pathology other than neoplasia should conta in 
20 animals of each sex while the satellite control group should contain 10 
animals of each sex. Observation parameters should include signs of 
intoxication, effect on body weight, food intake, clinical chemistry parameters, 
hematology parameters, urine analysis, organ weights, gross pathology and 
detailed histopathology. Comprehensive descriptions of benign and malignant 
tumour development, time of their detection, site, dimensions, histological 
typing etc. should be given.
