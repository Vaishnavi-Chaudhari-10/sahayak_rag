The aims of this study
This study analyses the choices available to policymakers 
regarding patent disclosure requirements related to genetic 
resources and traditional knowledge. It is not intended to 
promote patent disclosure requirements related to genetic 
resources and traditional knowledge, and does not advo-
cate any particular approach to implementing them. On 
the contrary, its aim is to emphasize the variety of issues 
at stake and the many options available, exploring and 
illustrating them through examples from different national
