3. Why was the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 enacted?

In compliance with our obligations under CBD, the Biological Diversity Act was enacted with the core objectives to conserve the biological resources, ensure sustainable use of its components, and promote the fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising from the utilisation of such biological resources and or associated knowledge
