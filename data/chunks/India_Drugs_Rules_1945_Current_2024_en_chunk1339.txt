actions. Self-inspections shall be performed routinely, and may be, in 
addition, performed on special occasions e.g., in the case of product 
recall or repeated rejections, or when an inspection by the regulatory 
authorities is announced. The team responsible for self -inspection 
shall consist of personnel who can evaluate the implementation of 
good manufacturing practices objectively. All recommendations for 
corrective action shall be implemented. The procedure for self -
inspection shall be documented and there shall be an effective follow -
up programme.
