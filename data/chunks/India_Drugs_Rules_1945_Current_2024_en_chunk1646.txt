3.2. Specific disposal systems shall be mandatory for radioactive effluents. 
These systems shall be effectively and carefully maintained to 
prevent contamination and exposure of personnel to the radioactive 
waste both within and outside the facility. 
3.3. Sinks shall be excluded from aseptic areas. Any sink installed in other 
clean areas shall be of suitable material and be regularly sanitised. 
Adequate precautions shall be taken to avoid contamination of the 
drainage system with radioactive effluents.
