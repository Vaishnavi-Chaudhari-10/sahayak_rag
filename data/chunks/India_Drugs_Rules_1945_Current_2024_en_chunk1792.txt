8.8. In-process controls shall include periodical checking of weight of bulk 
formulation filled in the containers. In a two -shot-filling process (liquid 
filling followed by gaseous filling), it shall be ensured that one hun dred 
per cent check on weight is carried out. 
8.9. Controls after filling shall ensure the absence of undue leakage. Any 
leakage test shall be performed in a way which avoids microbial 
contamination or residual moisture. 
8.10. Filled containers shall be quarantined for a suitable period established by 
the manufacturer to detect leaking containers prior to testing, labelling 
and packing.
