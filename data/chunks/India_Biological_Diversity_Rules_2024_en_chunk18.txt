On being satisfied with the merit of the application, the Authority may grant the approval for access to biological resources and associated knowledge subject to such term and conditions as it may deem fit to impose.
The approval to access shall be in the form of a written agreement duly signed by an authorized officer of the Authority and the applicant.
The form of the agreement referred to in sub-rule (5) shall be laid down by the Authority and shall include the following ; namely :-
general objectives and purpose of the application for seeking approval ;
description of the biological resources and traditional knowledge including accompanying information;
intended uses of the biological resources (research, breeding, commercial utilization etc.)
