Chapter-13: Revocation of Patent 
13.01 Revocation of Patent 
13.01.01 Revocation before the High Court or Appellate Board 
a. Any person interested or the Central Government may make a petition 
on any of the grounds, specified for revocation of Patent under 
Section 64 of the Patents Act, before the IPAB. A Patent may also be 
revoked by the High Court on a counter -claim in a suit for 
infringement of patent. 
b. Grounds for revocation of the patent before the IPAB as well as the 
High Court are elaborated in Section 64.
