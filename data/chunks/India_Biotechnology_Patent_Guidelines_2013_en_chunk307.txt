13.01.04 Revocation by Controller for non-working 
a. Where, in respect of a patent, a compulsory licens e has been granted, 
the Central Government or any person interested may, after the 
expiration of two years from the date of the order granti ng the first 
compulsory license, apply to the Controller for an order revoking the 
patent on the ground: 
i. that the patented invention has not been wor ked in the 
territory of India, or 
ii. that reasonable requirements of the public with respect to the 
patented invention have not been satisfied, or 
iii. that the patented invention is not available to the public at a 
reasonably affordable price.
