 Legal jurisprudence: 
Indian Courts while deciding the petitions on non-compliance of 
section 8 requirement by the patentee/applicant have analyzed the 
matter. The gist of rulings by the Courts is as under: 
 the provision for revocation of patent under section 64(1) (m) 
on the ground of non -compliance of Section 8 should not be 
exercised solely and automat ically just because it exists in the 
Act, as there lies a discretion in the Court not to revoke the 
patent on the peculiar facts and circumstances of the present 
case. The said discretion exists by use of the word, ―may‖ 
under Section 64 of the Act.
