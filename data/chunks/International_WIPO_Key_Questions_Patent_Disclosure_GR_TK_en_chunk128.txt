Box 18: Contract law: “derived product” under MTAs
An MTA will commonly establish a contractual 
relationship between provider and user, and this 
will often govern subsequent use of material 
derived from the GR as received (including 
ownership, licensing or other aspects of patent 
rights on products derived from the GR). This leads 
to a wide range of approaches to characterizing the 
link between GR or TK and a patented invention, 
including in terms of a “derivative product”.
