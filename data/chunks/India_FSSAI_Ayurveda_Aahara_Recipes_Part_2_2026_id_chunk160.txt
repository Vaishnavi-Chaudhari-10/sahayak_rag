Method of 
preparation 
Take all the herbs in quantity ,coarsely powder them and mix 
together. Boil the herbal powder in 16 parts of water until 8 parts of 
water remains. Strain the decoction through a cotton cloth into a 
vessel. Add laja to the decoction in a ratio of 1:14. Cook over 
medium heat until the Laja is properly cooked/softened. Collect the 
Laja along with the supernatant water in a separate vessel. Add 
Navneeta to it, and finally, make it sour in taste by adding 
Vrikshamla rasa, Dadimarasa, Amlika, and Kolamlarasa.
