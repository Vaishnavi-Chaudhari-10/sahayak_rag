I. Condoms 
1. Description—Condoms consist of cylindrical rubber sheaths with one 
end open. The open end shall terminate with an integral rim. The closed end 
may have a receptacle. They may be supplied rolled and shall be free from 
tackiness and shall be capable of being unrolled readily.
