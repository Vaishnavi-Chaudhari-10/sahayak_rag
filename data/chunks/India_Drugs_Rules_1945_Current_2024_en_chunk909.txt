H. Storage of Finished Product 
(i) The final products shall be stored between two degree centigrade to eight 
degree centigrade, unless otherwise specified by the Central Licence Approving 
Authority. 
(ii) The shelf-life assigned to the products by the licensee shall be submitted for 
approval to the licensing authority and Central Licence Approving Authority. 
I. Labelling 
The products manufactured shall be labelled as specified in the Indian 
Pharmacopoeia, the British Pharmacopoeia or the United States' Pharmacopoeia 
which shall be in addition to any other requirement stated under part IX or Part 
X of these rules. The labels shall indicate the results of tests for 1082[Hepatitis B
