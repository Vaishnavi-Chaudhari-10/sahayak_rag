19.4.1. Other QC responsibilities include the following, namely:- 
(a) establishing, validating and implementing all QC procedures; 
(b) evaluating, maintaining and storing reference standards for 
substances; 
(c) ensuring the correct labelling of containers of materials and 
products; 
(d) ensuring that the stability of the active pharmaceutical 
ingredients and products are monitored; 
(e) participating in the investigation of complaints related to the 
quality of the product; 
(f) participating in environmental monitoring; and 
(g) participation in quality risk management programme. 
19.4.2. These activities shall be carried out in accordance with written 
procedures and, where necessary, recorded.
