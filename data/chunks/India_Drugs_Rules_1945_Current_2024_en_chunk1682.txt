14.3. Non-wooden equipment shall be used unless tradition demands 
wooden material. Where it is necessary to use traditional equipment 
(such as wooden implements, clay pots, pallets, hoppers, etc.), this 
shall be dedicated, unless otherwise justified. When such equipment 
is used, it is advisable that it does not come into direct contact with 
chemicals or contaminated material. If the use of wooden equipment 
is unavoidable, special consideration must be given to its cleaning as 
wooden materials may retain odours, be easily discoloured and are 
easily contaminated.
