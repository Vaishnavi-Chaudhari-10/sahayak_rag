17.3.1.2. All finished drugs shall be identified by labelling 
bearing at least the following information, namely:- 
(a) the name of the drugs; 
(b) a list of the active ingredients [if applicable, with 
the International Nonproprietary Names (INN)], 
showing the amount of each present and a 
statement of the net contents (e.g., number of 
dosage units, weight and volume); 
(c) the batch number assigned by the manufacturer; 
(d) the exp iry date and date of manufacture in an 
uncoded form; 
(e) any special storage conditions or handling 
precautions that may be necessary; 
(f) directions for use, and warnings and precautions
