1.8. Correct directional air movement and a pressure cascade system can 
assist in preventing cross -contamination. The pressure cascade shall be 
such that th e direction of airflow is from the clean corridor into the 
cubicles, resulting in dust containment. 
1.9. The corridor shall be maintained at a higher pressure than the cubicles, 
and the cubicles at a higher pressure than atmospheric pressure. 
1.10. Highly potent prod ucts shall be manufactured under a pressure cascade 
regime that is negative relative to atmospheric pressure. 
1.11. The pressure cascade for each facility shall be individually assessed 
according to the product handled and level of protection required. 
1.12. Building structure shall be given special attention to accommodate the 
pressure cascade design.
