1.5.3 Before applying for marketing approval 
Follow-up and supplemental safety pharmacology studies should be 
assessed prior to approval unless not required, in which case this should 
be justified. Available informat ion from toxicology studies addressing 
safety pharmacology endpoints or information from clinical studies can 
replace such studies. 
1.6 Application of Good Laboratory Practices (GLP) 
The animal studies be conducted in an accredited laboratory. Where the 
safety pharmacology studies are part of toxicology studies, these studies should 
also be conducted in an accredited laboratory.
