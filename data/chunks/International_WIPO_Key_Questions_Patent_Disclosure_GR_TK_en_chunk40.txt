However, information about the origin and/or source of GRs 
and TK may be voluntarily disclosed in a patent application 
if the applicant believes that it would be needed to meet the 
requirements for patentability such as novelty, 12 inventive 
step13 and industrial applicability. 14 In these cases, such 
information could be considered to be “material” to the 
patentability of the claimed invention.15 For instance, access 
to samples of a GR may be necessary to enable a “person 
skilled in the art” to practice the claimed invention without 
undue experimentation.
