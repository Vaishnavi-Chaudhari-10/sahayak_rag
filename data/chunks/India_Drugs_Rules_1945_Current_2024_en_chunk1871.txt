11. Laboratory controls:- 
11.1. General controls- 
11.1.1. The independent quality units shall have at its disposal 
adequate laboratory facilities. 
11.1.2. There shall be documented procedures describing sampling, 
testing, approval or rejection of materials and recording and 
storage of laboratory data. Laboratory records shall be 
maintained in accordance with paragraph 6.6. 
11.1.3. All speciﬁcations, sampling plans and test procedures shall be 
scientiﬁcally sound and appropriate to ensure that raw 
materials, intermediates, APIs, labels and packaging materials 
conform to establishe d standards of quality and purity. 
Speciﬁcations and test procedures shall be consistent with those 
included in the registration or ﬁling. There can be speciﬁcations 
in addition to those in the registration or ﬁling. Speciﬁcations,
