Article 21
The description must contain a clear indication 
of the origin of the genetic or biological 
resources collected in the territory of 
Burundi and used directly or indirectly 
in the making of the claimed invention 
as well as any element of the traditional 
knowledge which may or may not be 
linked to these resources that is protected 
under Title V of this part and that has been 
used directly or indirectly in the making of the 
claimed invention without the prior informed 
consent of its individual or joint creators.
