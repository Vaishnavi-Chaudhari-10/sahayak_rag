(b) Identification—It causes characteristic cytopathic effect in dog, pig 
and ferret kidney monolayers. This can be neutralised by specific 
antiserum. When inoculated into dogs, the development of specific 
neutralizing antibodies can be demonstrated by suitable serological tests. 
(c) Moisture content—In the finished product moisture content shall not 
exceed 1.0 per cent. 
(d) Sterility test—Shall comply with the tests for sterility as described 
under the general monograph on "Viral Vaccines".
