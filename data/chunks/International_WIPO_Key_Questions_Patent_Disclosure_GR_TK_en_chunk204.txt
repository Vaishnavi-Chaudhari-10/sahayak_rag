Examination capacity
Many developing countries’ patent offices do not have the full 
capacity to undertake a substantive examination of patent 
applications. Thus, these developing countries often opt for 
a simple patent registration with only formality examination 
of patent applications, utilization of substantive examination 
reports for the corresponding foreign patent application 
(containing the same invention) prepared by other patent 
offices, or join a system of regional or international cooper-
ation, such as the African Intellectual Property Organization 
(Organisation Africaine de la Propriété Intellectuelle, OAPI)3 
and ARIPO4 in Africa and the Eurasian Patent Organization5 
in Eastern Europe and Central Asia, where substantive 
patent examinations are conducted through a centralized 
mechanism.
