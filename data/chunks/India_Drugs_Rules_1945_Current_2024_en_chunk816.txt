5. Clinical Documentation 

A new drug as defined under rule 122E of the Drugs and Cosmetics 
Rules, 1945 is required to be permitted separately by the licensing authority 
under rule 122A of the said rules prior to its registration. Such a new drug 
requires a brief summary on clinical documentation, along with permission 
under 122A of the said rules for its Registration Certificate.
