4.5. The sampling process shall not adversely affect the quality of the 
product. Incoming starting materials shall be sampled under 
appropriate conditions in order to prevent contamination and cross -
contamination. 
4.6. Where justified (such as the special case of sterile starting materials) it 
may be acceptable to reduce the risk of contamination by not 
performing sampling at the time of receipt but to perform the testing 
later, on the samples taken at the time of use. In such cases, release of 
the finished product is conditional upon satisfactory results of these 
tests.
