13.   Powers and duties of  Chairperson
The Chairperson shall have the overall control of the day- to -day activities of the Authority.
Subject to the provisions of Section 10, the Chairperson shall have the powers of general superintendence over the officers and staff of the Authority and he may issue necessary directions for the conduct and management of the affairs of the Authority.
