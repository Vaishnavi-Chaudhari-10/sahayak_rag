6. Storage—Live viral vaccines shall be stored , protected from light at 
sub-zero temperature as required. Other viral vaccines shall be stored at 2°C to 
4°C but shall not be frozen. 
7. Condition of housing of animals —(i) The animals used in the 
production of vaccine must be housed in hygienic condi tions in premises 
satisfactory for this purpose.
