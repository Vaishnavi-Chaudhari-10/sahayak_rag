12.5. Cleaning validation shall be performed in order to confirm the 
effectiveness of cleaning procedures designed to remove biological 
substances, growth media, process reagents, cleaning agents, 
inactivation agents and so on. Careful consideration shall be given to 
cleaning validation when campaign-based production is practiced. 
12.6. Critical processes for inactivation or elimination of poten tially 
harmful microorganisms of Biosafety Risk Group 2 or above, 
including genetically modified ones, are subject to validation.
