H. Criteria for Blood Donation 
Conditions for donation of blood: 
(1) General—No person shall donate blood and no blood bank shall draw 
blood from a person, more than once in three months. The donor shall be in 
good health, mentally alert and physically fit and shall not be inmates of jail, 
persons having multiple sex partners and drug -addicts. The donors shall fulfil 
the following requirements, namely:— 
1071[(a) the donor shall be in the age group of 18 to 65 years;] 
(b) the donor shall not be less than 45 kilograms; 
(c) temperature and pulse of the donor shall be normal; 
(d) the systolic an d diastolic blood pressures are within normal limits 
without medication; 
(e) haemoglobin which shall not be less than 12.5 grams; 
(f) the donor shall be free from acute respiratory diseases; 
(g) the donor shall be free from any skin diseases at th e site of
