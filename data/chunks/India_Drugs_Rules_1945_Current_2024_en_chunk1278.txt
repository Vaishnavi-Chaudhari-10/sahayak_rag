5. Chemicals and Reagents: 
(a) The storage and handling of chemicals and reagents shall be done in 
a manner considering the physicochemical properties of these 
substances and the hazards involved in their use. 
(b) All reagents and solutions in the laboratory shall be propert y 
identified with a label. 
(c) A standardisation register shall be maintained by the laboratory 
along with its raw data and Standard Operating Procedure for 
preparation and standardisation on stock solutions, standard 
solutions, volumetric solutions must be prepared for the guidance of 
staff. 
(d) Containers of stock solutions and of standard solutions shall bear the 
following details:— 
(i) name of analytical chemist who prepared the solution; 
(ii) date of preparation; 
(iii) Each volumetric solu tion shall have "use before date" 
depending upon the stability of the solution; and
