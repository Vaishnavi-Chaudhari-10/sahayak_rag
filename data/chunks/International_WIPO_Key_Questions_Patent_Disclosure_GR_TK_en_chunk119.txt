Philippines: Rule 12 Section 3(c) of the Implementing 
Rules and Regulations of Republic Act No. 10055 
(Joint Administrative Order No. 02-2010) provides: 
“The disclosure requirement under this section shall 
apply when the subject matter contained in a national 
or international IPR [intellectual property rights] appli-
cation is directly based on any biodiversity, genetic 
resources or materials, traditional knowledge, and 
indigenous knowledge, systems and practices to 
which the research and development institutes and/
or institutions have had access prior to the filing of 
the IPR application. The subject matter contained 
in the IPR application must depend on the specific 
properties of, or must be consciously derived from, 
such biodiversity and genetic resources or materials, 
traditional knowledge, and indigenous knowledge, 
systems and practice.”
Accordingly, some legal systems further extend the scope 
of disclosure
