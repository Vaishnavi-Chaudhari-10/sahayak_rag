4.7. In the case of labelling kits, freeze drying shall be carried out as an 
aseptic procedure. If an inert gas such as nitrogen is used to fill vials, it 
must be filtered to remove possible microbial contamination. 
4.8. The dispensing, packaging and transportation of radiopharmaceuticals 
shall comply with the relevant provisions of the Atomic Energy Act 
1962 and the rules made thereunder. 

5. Labelling:- 
5.1. All products shall be clearly identified by labels, which must remain 
permanently attached to the containers under all storage conditions. An 
area of the container shall be left uncovered to allow inspection of the 
contents. If the final container is not suitable for labelling, the label shall 
appear on its package.
