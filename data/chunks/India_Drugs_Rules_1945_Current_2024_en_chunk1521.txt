7.5. Changing and washing shall follow a written procedure designed to 
minimise the contamination of clean -area clothing or the carry -through 
of contaminants to clean areas. The clothing and its quality shall be 
appropriate for the process and the grade of the working area. It shall be 
worn in such a way so as to protect the product from contamination.

Grade B and C rooms. For every worker in a Grade A or B area, clean 
sterile (sterilised or adequately sanitized) protective garments shall be
