12. Whether access to biological resources, which are normally traded as commodities (NTAC), is exempted from NBA approval for IPR?

No, permission is required for the biological resources notified as NTACs if they are utilized in IPR as per Section 40.

13. What is "digital sequence information"?

Digital Sequence Information (DSI) refers to data of genetic information from biological resources, including DNA, RNA, and protein sequences, and other related molecular data.
