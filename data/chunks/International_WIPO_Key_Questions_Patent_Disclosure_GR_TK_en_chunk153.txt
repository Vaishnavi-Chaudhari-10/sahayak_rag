The expression “[…] utilization of genetic 
resources as well as subsequent applications 
and commercialization” is used to trigger fair 
and equitable benefit-sharing obligations under 
Article 5 of the Nagoya Protocol. In addition, 
patent/IP offices may be designated as possible 
checkpoints under Nagoya Protocol Article 17. 
These “[…] checkpoints would collect or receive, as 
appropriate, relevant information related to […] the 
utilization of genetic resources, as appropriate.”
