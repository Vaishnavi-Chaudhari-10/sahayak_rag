10. Storage and distribution:- 
10.1. Warehousing procedures- 
10.1.1. Facilities shall be available for the storage of all materials under 
appropriate conditions (e.g., controlled temperature and 
humidity when necessary). Records shall be maintained of these 
conditions, if they are critical for the maintenance of material 
characteristics. 
10.1.2. Unless there is an alternative system to prevent the unintentional 
or unauthorised use of quarantined, rejected, returned or recalled 
materials, separate storage areas shall b e assigned for their 
temporary storage until the decision as to their future use has 
been taken. 
10.2. Distribution procedures- 
10.2.1. APIs and intermediates shall only be released for distribution to 
third parties after they have been released by the quality units.
