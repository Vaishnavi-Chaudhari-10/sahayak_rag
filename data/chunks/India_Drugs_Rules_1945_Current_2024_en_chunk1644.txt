2.4. During the working day, personnel may pass between radioactive and 
non-radioactive areas only if the safety rules of radiation control (he alth 
physics control) are followed. 
2.5. The release of a batch may be approved only by an authorised person or a 
person with academic qualifications officially registered as a suitably 
qualified person, and with appropriate experience in the manufacture of 
radiopharmaceuticals. 
2.6. To ensure the safe manufacture of radiopharmaceuticals, personnel shall 
be trained in GMP, the safe handling of radioactive materials and 
radiation safety procedures. They shall also be required to take periodic 
courses and receive training to keep abreast of the latest developments in 
their fields.

effectiveness of training programmes
