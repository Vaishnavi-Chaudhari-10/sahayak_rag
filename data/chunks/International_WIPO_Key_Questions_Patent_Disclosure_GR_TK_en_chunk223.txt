In addition to the above reporting obligations, countries 
are free to choose whether or not to subject any PGRFA- 
related inventions to a specific disclosure requirement 
under patent law. 
The ITPGRFA is one sectorially specific, specialized ABS 
agreement, which is recognized in the Nagoya Protocol. 
The Preamble of the Protocol explicitly “recall[s that] the 
Multilateral System of Access and Benefit-sharing [was] 
established under the International Treaty on Plant Genetic 
Resources for Food and Agriculture developed in harmo -
ny with the Convention”. Article 4 of the Nagoya Protocol 
furthermore provides that in general the Protocol “shall be 
implemented in a mutually supportive manner with other
