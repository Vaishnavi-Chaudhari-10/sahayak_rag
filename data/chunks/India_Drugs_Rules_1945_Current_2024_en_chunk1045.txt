4. Standards— 
(a) Description—Light cream coloured powder. 
(b) Identification—This product when applied to scarified area of the skin of 
the sheep or goats produces characteristic local lesions of pox and s hould afford 
protection to sheep and goat against Sheep and Goat Pox. 
(c) Safety Test —Two rabbits each weighing not less than 1 kg are injected 
subcutaneously each with 1 ml. of a 1:100 dilution of the vaccine in normal 
saline solution. These animals ar e observed for fourteen days. The animals 
should remain normal.

general monograph on 'Viral Vaccines'.
