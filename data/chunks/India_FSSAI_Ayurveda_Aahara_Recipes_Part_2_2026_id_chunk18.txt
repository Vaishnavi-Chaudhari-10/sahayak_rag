Method of 
preparation 
Wash the rice with lukewarm water. Take washed rice in a stainless 
steel vessel and add three times water. Put the stainless steel vessel 
on the fire. As the rice begins to cook and the middle part of rice is 
still slightly firm, add Takra, Dugdha or water as required . Stir it 
frequently with a ladle. Once cooked, remove the stainless steel 
vessel from the fire and place it on hot charcoal (Angara). Allow it to 
cook slowly until the rice becomes soft.
