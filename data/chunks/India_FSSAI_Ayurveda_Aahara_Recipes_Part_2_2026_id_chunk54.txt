Method of 
preparation 
Collect the fresh leaves of Punarnava. Boil in required quantity of 
water along with Karav alli leaves and the powder of S hunthi to 
remove bitterness. After the bitterness is removed, mix the leaves 
with powdered spices and salt keep aside. Heat ghee in a pan, then 
add the Punarnava leaves and fry gently until lightly golden.
