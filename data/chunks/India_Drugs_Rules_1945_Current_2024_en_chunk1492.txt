5.9. Water sources, water -treatment equipment and treated water shall be 
monitored regularly for chemicals, biological contamination and 
contamination with endotoxins to ensure that the water complies with 
the specifications appropriate to its use. Records shall be maintained of 
the results of the monitoring and of any action taken.
