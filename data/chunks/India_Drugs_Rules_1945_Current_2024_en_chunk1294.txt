4. Protocols and specifications archive: 
(a) Every laboratory shall have a specification archive and current 
versions of all necessary specifications shall be kept as per the 
requirements of the Act and the rules made thereunder and the National 
Pharmacopoeia (Indian Pharmacopoeia). 
(b) All updates and corrections must be noted in the mas ter volumes of 
Pharmacopoeias to prevent the use of obsolete sections; supplement and 
addendum shall also be made available in the laboratory.
