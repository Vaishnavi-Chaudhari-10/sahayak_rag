Demand for International Preliminary Examination 
1. The demand for international preliminary examination shall be 
made separately from the international application. 
2. The demand for international preliminary examination may be 
made to the Indian Patent Office, Delhi, International Bureau or to 
any of the six competent International Preliminary Examining 
Authorities mentioned above.
