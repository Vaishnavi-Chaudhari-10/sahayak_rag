5. Garments:- 
5.1. Personnel in the manufacturing and filling section shall wear suitable 
single piece garment made out of non -shedding, tight weave material. 
Personnel in support areas shall wear clean factory uniforms. 
5.2. Gloves made of suitable material having no interaction with the 
propellants shall be used by the operators in the manufacturing and 
filling areas. Preferably, disposable gloves shall be used. 
5.3. Suitable department specific PPE like footwear and safety glasses shall 
be used, wherever hazard exists.
