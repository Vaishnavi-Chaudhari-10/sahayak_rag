Brazil: Article 47 of Law No. 13.123 of May 20, 2015 
(Access and Benefits Sharing of Genetic Resources 
and Associated Traditional Knowledge) provides: 
“The grant of intellectual property rights by the 
competent body for the final product or for the 
reproductive material obtained through access to 
genetic resources or associated traditional knowledge 
shall be subject to the registration or authorization 
provided for in this Law.”
