15.04 Acquisition of inventions and patents by the Central government 
a. The Central Government may, if satisfied that it is necessary that an 
invention which is the subject of an application for a patent or a 
patent should be acquired from the applicant or the patentee for a 
public purpose, publish a notificati on to that effect in the Official 
Gazette, and thereupon the invention or patent and all rights in 
respect of the invention or patent shall, by force of this section, stand 
transferred to and be vested in the Central Government.
