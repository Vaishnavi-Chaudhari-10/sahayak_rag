12.6.2. Premises shall preferably be laid out in such a way so as to 
allow the production to take place in areas connected in a 
logical order corresponding to the sequence of the operations 
and to the requisite cleanliness levels. 
12.6.3. The adequacy of t he working and in -process storage space 
shall permit the orderly and logical positioning of equipment 
and materials so as to minimise the risk of confusion between 
different pharmaceutical products or their components, to avoid 
cross-contamination and to m inimise the risk of omission or 
wrong application of any of the manufacturing or control steps.
