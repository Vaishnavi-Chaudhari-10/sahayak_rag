8.4.3. Acceptable blending operations include but are not limited to- 
(i) blending of small batches to increase batch size; 
(I) blending of tailings (i.e., relatively small quantities of 
isolated material) from batches of the same intermediate 
or API to form a single batch. 
8.4.4. Blending processes shall be adequately controlled and 
documented and the blended batch sha ll be tested for 
conformance to established speciﬁcations, where appropriate. 
8.4.5. The batch record of the blending process shall allow traceability 
back to the individual batches that make up the blend.
