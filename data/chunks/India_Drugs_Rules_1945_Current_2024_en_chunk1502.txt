6.11.1.2. Sufficient time shall be allowed for the whole of the load to 
reach the required temperature before measurement of the 
sterilising time is started. This time shall be determined for

6.11.1.3. After the high -temperature phase of a heat sterilisation cycle, 
precautions shall be taken against contamination of a sterilised 
load during cooling. Any cooling fluid or gas in contact with 
the product shall be sterilised.
