“30AA. Import of New Homoeopathic medicines. — (1) No New Homoeopathic medicine shall be imported except 
under and in accordance with the permission in writing by the Licensing Authority as defined in clause (b) of rule 21. 
(2) The importer of a New Homoeopathic medicine when applying for permission under sub -rule (1) shall produce 
before the Licensing Authority such documentary and other evidence as may be required by the Licensing Authority 
for assessing the safety, therapeutic efficacy of the medicine including the minimum homoeopathic provings carried 
out with it.
