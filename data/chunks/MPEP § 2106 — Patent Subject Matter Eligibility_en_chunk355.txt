 exception does not mean that the claim must be narrow in order to be eligible. The
 courts have identified some broad claims as eligible see, 
e.g.,
McRO, Inc. v. Bandai Namco Games Am. Inc.,
 837 F.3d 1299, 120
 USPQ2d 1091 (Fed. Cir. 2016); 
Thales Visionix Inc. v. United
 States,
 850 F.3d. 1343, 121 USPQ2d 1898 (Fed. Cir. 2017), and some
 narrow claims as ineligible see 
e.g.,
Ultramercial, Inc. v. Hulu, LLC,
 772 F.3d 709, 112 USPQ2d 1750
 (Fed. Cir. 2014); 
Electric Power Group, LLC v. Alstom, S.A.,

 F.3d 1350, 119 USPQ2d 1739 (Fed. Cir. 2016). Thus, examiners should carefully
