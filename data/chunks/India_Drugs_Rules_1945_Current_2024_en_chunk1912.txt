17.1.3. The term “classical fermentation” refers to the processes that 
use microorganisms existing in nature or modiﬁed by 
conventional methods (e.g., irradiation or chemical 
mutagenesis) to produce APIs. APIs produced by “classical 
fermentation” are normally low molecular weight products 
such as antibiotics, amino acids, vitamins, and carbohydrates.
