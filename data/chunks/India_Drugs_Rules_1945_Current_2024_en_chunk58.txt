82[36A.Import of drugs by charitable hospital free of cost. —(1) Small 
quantity of drugs received in donation by a charitable hospital for the purpose of 
treatment of the patients in the said hospital may be imported provided the 
drugs are given or administered to the patients free of cost. 
(2) The drugs shall not be prohibited for import and permitted to be marketed 
in the country with residual shelf life of one year or more.]
