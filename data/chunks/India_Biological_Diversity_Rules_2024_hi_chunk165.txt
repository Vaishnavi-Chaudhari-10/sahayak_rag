23. Manner of inquiry by the Adjudicating Officer .- (1) No Adjudicating Officer shall inquire into a complaint of 
non-compliance or contravention of the provisions referred to in section 55 of the Act unless the complaint is filed by 
or on behalf of the authorised officer, along with all necessary docume nts, either on its own motion or on receipt of a 
representation. 
(2) The complaint may be represented by a presenting officer who shall be a legal practitioner and shall appear and 
present the case before the Adjudicating Officer, on behalf of the authorised officers, within their respective jurisdiction. 
(3) The presenting officer shall, before forwarding the matter under sub-rule(1) to the Adjudicating Officer, process the 
same exercising reasonable due diligence, and bring on record all relevant facts and circumstances that need to be taken
