general conditions: —

approved institution) shall provide and maintain an adequate staff and adequate premises 
and equipment as specified in rule 150-C 2[and Schedule L-1]. 

(b) The approved institution s hall provide proper facilities for storage so as to 
preserve the properties of the samples to be tested by it.
