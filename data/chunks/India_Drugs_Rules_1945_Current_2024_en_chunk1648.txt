3.5. Ventilation of radiopharmaceutical production facilities shall meet the 
requirement to prevent the contamination of products and the exposure 
of working personnel to radioactivity. Suitable pressure and airflow 
patterns shall be maintained by appropriate isolation or enveloping 
methods. Air handling systems for both radioactive and non -radioactive 
areas shall be fitted with alarms so that the working personnel in the 
laboratory are warned of any failure of these systems. 
3.6. Dedicated facilities and equipment shall be u sed for the manufacture of 
any radiopharmaceutical product derived from human blood or plasma.

placed behind a lead shield to minimise the radiation exposure of the 
operators. Such auto claves shall be checked for contamination
