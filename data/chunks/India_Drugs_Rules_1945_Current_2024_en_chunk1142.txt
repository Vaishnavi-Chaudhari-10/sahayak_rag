1. Synonym—Milk Ring Test Antigen.

standard strain of Brucella abortus strain 99 strained supravitally with 2, 3, 5, 
triphenyl tetrazolium chloride suspended in 0.85 per cent saline containing 1 per 
cent glycerol and 1 per cent phenol.
