11.3.6. The authorised person is responsible for compliance with 
technical or regulatory requirements related to the quality of 
finished products and the approval for the release of finished 
product for sale or supply. 
11.3.7. Assessment of finished products shall embrace all relevant 
factors, including the production conditions, the results of in -
process testing, the manufacturing (including packaging) 
documentation, compliance with the specification for the 
finished product and an examination of the finished pack. 
11.3.8. No batch of product is to be released for sale or supply prior to 
certification by the authorised persons.
