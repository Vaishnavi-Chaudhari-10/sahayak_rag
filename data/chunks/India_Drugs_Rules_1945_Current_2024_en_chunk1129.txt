ANTI-ANTHRAX SERUM 
1. Synonym—Bacillus Anthracis Anti-serum. 
2. Definition—Anti-axthrax serum is the serum of animals that confers a 
specific protection against Bacillus anthracis. 
3. Preparation—The anti -serum may be prepared in buffalo bulls after 
repeated injections of cultures of B. Anthracis of a virulent strain. 
4. Standard—It complies with the requirements in the general provisions 
for anti -sera under Description, Acidity or Alkalinity, Abnormal Toxicity, 
Sterility Solids, Labelling, Storage and Expiry date. 
Identification—It protects animals against infection with B. Anthracis.
