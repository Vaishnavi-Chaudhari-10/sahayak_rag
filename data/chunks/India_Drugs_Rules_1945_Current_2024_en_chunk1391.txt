15.6. Reference standards shall be properly labelled with at least the 
following information, namely- 
(a) name of the material; 
(b) batch or lot number and control number; 
(c) date of preparation; 
(d) shelf-life; 
(e) potency; and 
(f) storage conditions. 
15.7. All in -house working standards or secondary standards shall be 
standardised against an official reference standard, when available, 
initially and at regular intervals thereafter. 
15.8. All reference standards shall be stored and used in a manner that will 
not adversely affect their quality. 

16. Waste materials: 
16.1. Provision shall be made for the proper and safe storage of waste 
materials waiting disposal. Toxic substances and flammable materials 
shall be stored in suitably designed, separate, enclosed cupboards. 
16.2. Waste material shall not be allowed to accumulate. It shall be collected
