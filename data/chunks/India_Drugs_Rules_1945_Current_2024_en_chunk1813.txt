4.1.1. Buildings and facilities used in the manufacture of intermediates 
and APIs shall be located, designed and constru cted to facilitate

minimise potential contamination. Where microbiological

placement of equipment and materials to prevent mix -ups and 
contamination.

provides adequate protection of the material, such equipment can 
be located outdoors.

facilities shall be designed to prevent mix-ups or contamination.
