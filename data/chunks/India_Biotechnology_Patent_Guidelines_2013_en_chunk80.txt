05.02.02 Contents of Provisional Specification: 
Provisional s pecification is a techno -legal document which describes 
the invention and it is followed by a complete specification. 
a) A provisional specification shall essentially contain the title and 
description of the invention. 
It is advi sable not to include claims in the provisional 
specification as the purpose of filing a provisional specification 
is to claim a priority date and it is only meant for describing the 
invention. 
b) The description starts from the second page with the field of 
invention, background of the invention, object of the invention 
and statement of the invention.
