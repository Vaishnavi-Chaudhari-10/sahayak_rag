to transparency with regard to the geographical 
origin of the GR on which the invention is directly 
based. The standard form for national patent 
applications provides for tick boxes that oblige 
the applicant to declare (Yes or No) whether use 
has been made of GRs in the sense specified in 
Article 15, § 1, 6 BPL. The applicant is thus invited 
to provide information on the geographical source 
of the GR. If this information is not available, he 
or she may declare that the source is not known. 
This measure provides for a mere formality that 
does not put any burden on the patent office, i.e., 
the office does not have to undertake any further 
research regarding the geographical source 
of the material as declared by the applicant. 
Such information is made available to the 
public by means of inclusion of the application 
form in the public part of the patent file.
Source: WIPO/GRTKF/IC/16/INF/15, Annex, p.2.
