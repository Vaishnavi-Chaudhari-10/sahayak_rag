Guidelines are a non-binding instrument that 
provides an illustration of possible approaches 
to national ABS regulatory systems. They also 
provide some guidance on the possible interactions 
between the IP system and the CBD. In particular, 
they suggest measures to support compliance 
with ABS requirements, including “measures to 
encourage disclosure of the country of origin of 
genetic resources and of the origin of traditional 
knowledge, innovations and practices of 
indigenous and local communities in applications 
for intellectual property rights”, measures to 
prevent use of GRs obtained without PIC, and 
measures discouraging unfair trade practices.
