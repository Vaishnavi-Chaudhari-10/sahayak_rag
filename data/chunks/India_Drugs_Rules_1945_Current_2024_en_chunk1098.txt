4. Standards—It complies with the requirements of general stan dards of 
viral vaccine: 
(a) Description—Dry light yellow coloured flakes readily soluble in 
chilled saline or buffered saline. 
(b) Identifications—(i) Protects cattle against a subsequent challenge with 
virulent or caprinised rinderpest virus. 
(ii) It is titrable in tissue culture systems capable of supporting 
the multiplication of this virus. The test shall be made on at least 
three separate occasions using a cell culture derived from different 
animals. 
(iii) Specificity test shall be performed using an appropriate 
serum neutralisation test. 
(c)Sterility test—Each batch shall be tested for bacterial and mycotic 
sterility as given in the general monograph on "Viral Vaccines".
