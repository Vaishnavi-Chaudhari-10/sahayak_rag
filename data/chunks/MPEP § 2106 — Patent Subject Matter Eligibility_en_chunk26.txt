A process defines “actions”, 
i.e.,

 an invention that is claimed as an act or step, or a series of acts or steps. As
 explained by the Supreme Court, a “process” is “a mode of treatment of certain
 materials to produce a given result. It is an 
act, or a series of
 acts,
 performed upon the subject-matter to be transformed and reduced to
 a different state or thing.” 
Gottschalk v. Benson,

 70, 175 USPQ 673, 676 (1972) (italics added) (quoting
