03.04.01 Documents required for filing patent application 
A patent application should contain the following: 
1. Application for grant of patent in Form-1. 
2. A proof of right is required from the inventor(s) by way of 
endorsement in the appropriate paragraph of Form -1 or as an 
assignment from inventor(s) in favour of applicant(s), duly 
authenticated, irrespective of whether the appli cation is a 
Convention or National Phase application. Accordingly, it shall be 
filed within 6 months from the date of filing of the application in 
India. 
3. Provisional/complete specification in Form-2.
