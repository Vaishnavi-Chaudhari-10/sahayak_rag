21.2. The drying conditions chosen shall be appropriate to the type of plant 
material processed. This depends on both the character of the active 
ingredients (e.g., essential oils) and the type of plant part collected 
(e.g., root, leaf or flower). Drying by direct exposure to sunlight, if not 
specifically contraindicated, is possible, but drying on the ground shall 
be avoided. If the plant shall be processed fresh, without drying, the 
reasons and criteria determining the use of fresh material shall be 
stated.
