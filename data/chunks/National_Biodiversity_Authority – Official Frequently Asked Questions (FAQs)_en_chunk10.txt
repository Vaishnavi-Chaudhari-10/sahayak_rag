10. Does an Indian need approval to report a new species or genus?

Reporting a new species or genus is not a regulated activity under the provisions of the BD Act. However, prior intimation shall be provided to the designated repository in India.

11. How does the BD Act address the concerns relating to the protection of biodiversity-rich areas?

Section 37 (1) of the Act provides that based on the recommendations of the SBB, the State Government may, from time to time, notify in the Official Gazette, areas of biodiversity importance as Biodiversity Heritage Sites under this Act. Further, the SBB shall consult the local body and BMC concerned before making such recommendations.
