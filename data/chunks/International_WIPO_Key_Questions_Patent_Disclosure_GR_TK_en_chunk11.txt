Modern scientific research and the exploitation of genetic 
resources and traditional knowledge may offer great 
benefits to humankind. How can the patent system 
help scientists, commercial enterprises, public sector 
research institutions and civil society at large to realize 
those benefits while safeguarding the rights and interests 
of biodiversity-rich countries, innovators, indigenous 
and local communities and the scientific community?
It has been argued that new patent disclosure require -
ments related to genetic resources and traditional knowl-
edge are part of the answer, and several countries have 
already implemented such requirements in various forms.

priorities to this question. Policymakers in each country
