7.5. The licensing authorities shall be informed of any intention to recall 
the product because it is, or is suspected of being, defective. 
7.6. The distribution records shall be readily available to the authorised 
person, and they shall contain sufficient information on wholesalers 
and directly supplied customers to permit an effective recall. 
7.7. The progress of the recall process shall be monitored and recorded. 
Records shall include the disposition of the product. A final report 
shall be issued, including reconciliation between the delivered and 
recovered quantities of the products. 
7.8. The effectiveness of the arrangements for recall shall be tested and 
evaluated from time to time.
