6.4. Special attention shall be given to establishing that the product that 
gave rise to a complaint was defective. 
6.5. Any complaint concerning a product defect shall be recorded with all 
the original details and thoroughly investigated. The person 
responsible for Quality Control (QC) shall normally be involved in the 
review of such investigations. 
6.6. If a product defect is identified or suspected in a batch, consideration 
shall be given as to whether other batches shall be checked in order to 
determine whether they are also affected. In particular, other batches 
that may contain reprocessed product from the defective batch shall be 
investigated. 
6.7. Where necessary, appropriate follow -up action, possibly including 
product recall, shall be taken after investigation and evaluation of the 
complaint.
