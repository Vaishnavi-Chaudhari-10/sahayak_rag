23.4.5. To determine the shelf -life of finished products, strong 
emphasis shall also be placed on other tests in paragraph 18

23.4.6. The stability of preservatives and stabilisers shall be monitored. 
When these are not used, alternative tests shall be done to 
ensure that the product is self-preserving over its shelf-life. 
23.4.7. Samples used for stability studies shall be stored in the 
containers intended for marketing.
