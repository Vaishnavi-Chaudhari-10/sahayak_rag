06.02.02 Novelty and Inventive step 
An application for P atent of Addition cannot be challenged on the 
ground of lack of inventive step with respect to the disclosure in the 
main application or patent. But the disclosure in main application or 
patent may be cited for novelty against the application for patent 
of addition.
