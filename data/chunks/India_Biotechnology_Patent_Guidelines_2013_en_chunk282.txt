11.02.01 Procedure for disposal of application for Restoration 
1) When the Controller is prima facie satisfied after verification of 
evidence submitted in support of Form 15 that the failure to pay 
the renewal fee was unintentional and there had been no undue 
delay, the application for restoration will be published in the official 
journal under rule 84(3).
