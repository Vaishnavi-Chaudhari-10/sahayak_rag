4.7.2. It is recommended that a similar system be used for Grade B

by the effectiveness of the segregation between the adjacent Grade 
A and B zones. The Grade B zone shall be monitored at a

contamination and any deterioration of the system would be 
captured and alarms triggered if alert limits are exceeded.
