4  The WTO’s work on access to medicines and IP issues relating to public health is 
guided by the Doha Declaration on the TRIPS Agreement and Public Health; this 
clarifies the flexibilities in IP rules available to governments under the WTO’s 
Agreement on Trade Related Aspects of Intellectual Property Rights (TRIPS). 
See www.wto.org/english/tratop_e/trips_e/who_wipo_wto_e.htm .
