Method of 
preparation 
Take all the herbs in quantity ,coarsely powder them and mix 
together. Boil the herbal powder in 16parts of water until 8 parts of 
water remains. Strain the decoction through a cotton cloth into a 
vessel. Add rice to the decoction in a ratio of 1:14. Cook over 
medium heat until the rice is properly cooked/softened.
