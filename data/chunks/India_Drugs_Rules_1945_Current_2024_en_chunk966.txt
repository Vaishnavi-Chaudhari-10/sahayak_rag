FOWL CHOLERA VACCINE (POLYVALENT) 
1. Synonym—Pasteurella Septica Vaccine (Avian). 
2. Definition—Fowl Cholera Vaccine is a formolised pure broth culture of 
virulent strains of Pasteurella Septica (Avian). 
3. Preparation.—The strains are grown separately in nutrient broth for 48 
hours at 37°C. The pure growth is killed by the addition of a Solution of 
Formaldehyde I.P. in a suitable concentration. The cultures are then mixed in 
equal proportions and the final vaccine is bottled in suitable containers.

(a) Description—It is a light yellow liquid containing d ead bacteria 
in suspension. 
(b) Identification—It protects susceptible birds against P. aviseptica 
infection.
