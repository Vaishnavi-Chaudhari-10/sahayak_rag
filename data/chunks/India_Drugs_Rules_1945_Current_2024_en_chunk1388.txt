14.35. Recalled products shall be identified and stored separately in a secure 
area until a decision is taken and the decision shall be made as soon 
as possible. 
14.36. Products returned from the market shall be destroyed unless it is 
certain that their quality is satisfactory; in such cases they may be 
considered for resale or relabelling, or alternative action taken only 
after they have been critically assessed by the QC function in 
accordance with a written procedure. The nature of the product, any 
special storage conditions it requires, its condition and history, and 
the time elapsed since it was issued shall be taken into account in the 
assessment. Where any doubt arises over the quality of the product, it 
shall not be considered suitable for reissue or reuse. Any action taken
