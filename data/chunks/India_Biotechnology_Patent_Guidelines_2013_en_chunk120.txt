07.01.04 Documents to be submitted 
1) Every convention application shall: 
i. be accompanied by a complete specification; 
ii. specify the date on which the first of such applications was 
made and the convention country in which such application 
for protection is sought and 
iii. state that no application for protection in respect of the 
invention had been made in a convention country before that 
date by the applicant or by any person from whom he 
derives title. 
2) a complete specification filed with a convention application may 
include claims in respect of developments of, or additions to, the 
invent
