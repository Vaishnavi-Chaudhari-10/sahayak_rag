Box 10: Spotlight Patent term extensions
Under the World Trade Organization’s Agreement 
on Trade-Related Aspects of Intellectual Property 
Rights (TRIPS), patents expire 20 years after the 
initial filing date. Measures related to patent term 
extension go beyond the minimum standards 
which World Trade Organization (WTO) Members 
are obliged to implement under the TRIPS 
Agreement. However, such measures appear in U.S. 
law6 and the laws of some other countries that have 
free trade agreements with the U.S., for example 
Chile,7 Singapore8 and the Republic of Korea. 9
