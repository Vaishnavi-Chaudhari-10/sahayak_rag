17.2.4. Documents shall be regularly reviewed and kept up to date. 
When a document has been revised, a system shall exist to 
prevent inadvertent use of the superseded version. Superseded 
documents shall be retained for a specific period of time. 
17.2.5. Where documents require the entry of data, these entries shall 
be clear, legible and indelible. Sufficient space shall be 
provided for such entries.
