11.03 Registration of Assignments/Transfer of Right 
1) An assignment of a patent or of a share in a patent, a mortgage, 
licence or the creation of any other interest in a patent shall be valid 
only if the same were in writing and the agreement between the 
parties concerned is reduced to the form of a document embodying 
all the terms and conditions governing their rights and obligat ions 
and has been duly executed. 
2) Any person who becomes entitled by assignment, transmission or 
operation of law to a patent or to a share in patent or becomes 
entitled as a mortgagee, licensee or otherwise to any other interest in 
a patent, may apply in w riting in Form -16 to the Controller for the 
registration of his title or notice of his interest in the register , as the 
case may be . S
