12.4.8. There shall normally be a separate sampling area for starting

12.5. Weighing areas-The weighing of starting materials and the estimation 
of yield by weighing shall be carried out in separate weigh ing areas 
designed for that use, for example, with provisions for dust control. 
Such areas may be part of either storage or production areas.
