19.9.3. A written programme for on -going stability determination 
shall be developed and implemented to include elements 
such as — 
(a) a complete description of the drug involved in the study; 
(b) the complete set of testing parameters and methods, 
describing all tests for potency, purity, and physical 
characteristics and documented evidence that these tests 
indicate stability; 
(c) provision for the inclusion of a su fficient number of 
batches; 
(d) the testing schedule for each drug; 
(e) provision for special storage conditions; 
(f) provision for adequate sample retention; and 
(g) a summary of all the data generated, including the 
evaluation and the conclusion of the study. 
19.9.4. Stability shall be determined prior to marketing and 
following any significant changes, for example, in 
processes, equipment or packaging materials.
