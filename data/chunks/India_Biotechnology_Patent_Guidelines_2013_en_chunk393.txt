c. The affidavits required to be filed before the Controller of Patents 
under the Act or rules shall be duly sworn in the manner as 
prescribed under Clause (e) below. 
d. Affidavits shall be confined to such facts as the deponent is able, of 
his own knowledge, to prove except in interlocutory matters, where 
statements of belief of the deponent may be admitted, provided the 
grounds thereof are given. 
e. Affidavits shall be sworn to as follows: 
 i) In India - before any Court or person having by law authority 
to receive evidence, or before any officer empowered by such 
court as aforesaid to administer oaths or to take affidavits; 
 ii) In any country or place outside India - before a diplomatic or
