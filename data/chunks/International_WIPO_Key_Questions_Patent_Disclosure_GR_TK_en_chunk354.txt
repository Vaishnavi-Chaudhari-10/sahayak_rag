GENERAL PROVISIONS
FIRST
Prior to the granting of an intellectual 
property right, the Ecuadorian Intellectual 
Property Institute shall request the regis -
tration number of the access contract and a 
copy thereof, where there are reasonable 
indications or there is certainty that the 
products or processes whose protection is 
sought have been obtained from a genetic 
resource or a product derived therefrom, 
in accordance with the Constitution and the 
applicable law.
