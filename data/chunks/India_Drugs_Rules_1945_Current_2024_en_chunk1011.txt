1. Synonym—African Horse Sickness Vaccin e; Mouse adapted 
Polyvalent Horse Sickness Vaccine (Living). 
2. Definition—Horse sickness vaccine is a suspension of live mouse 
adapted strains of Horse Sickness Virus (onderstepoort) prepared from the 
brains of infected mice and is freeze dried. 
3. Preparation—Thirty to thirty -five-day-old white mice are infected 
intracerebrally with 0.05 ml. of a suitable dilution of the seed virus (6 or 7 
types, as the case may be). Groups of large numbers of mice are injected 
separately with each type of the virus and are housed at 27°C to 32°C. A 
majority of these become paralytic on the third and fourth day when they are 
scarified and their brains collected and stored at —15°C to —20°C till the day 
of processing. For preparing the polyvalent vaccine, equal number of brains 
collected from mice infected with different types of the virus are homogenised
