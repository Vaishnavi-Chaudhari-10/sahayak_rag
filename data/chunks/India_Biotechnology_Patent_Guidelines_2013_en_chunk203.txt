2. The Examiner shall assess if the claimed invention is capable of 
use in any industry or made using an industrial process . Typically, 
the specification explains the industrial applicability of the 
disclosed invention in a self -evident manner. If it is not, a mere 
suggestion that the matter would be industrially applicable is not 
sufficient. A specific utility should be indic ated in the specification 
supported by the disclosure. For example, indicating that a 
compound may be useful in treating unspecified disorders, or that 
the compound has useful biological ‖ properties, would not be 
sufficient to define a specific utility for the compound. The 
specific usefulness has to be indicated.
