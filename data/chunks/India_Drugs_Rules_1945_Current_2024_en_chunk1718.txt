Note.—Good Manufacturing Practices for pharmaceutical products: 
Main principles as given in Part I shall be complied for the manufacture 
of Investigational Pharmaceutical Products for Clinical Trials in Humans. 
In addition to these requirements, the following specific requirements 
shall also be followed, namely:— 
1. General considerations:- 
1.1. This Part supplements the general principle of GMP as specified in Part 
I and the guidelines on Good Clinical Practices (GCP) f or clinical trials 
on pharmaceutical products in India. The application of the principles of 
GMP to the preparation of investigational products to be used in Phase I 
or Phase II or Phase III of the clinical studies is necessary.
