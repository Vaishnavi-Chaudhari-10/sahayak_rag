Guiding 
Principle 4 
Discovering the Optimum or Workable range of traditionally known 
ingredients by routine experimentation may be considered inventive 
only if it is not obvious to the person skilled in the art. 
In case of inventi ons relating to selection of optimum or workable range of ingredients, 
such selection of a particular range of known ingredients is not inventive since the selection 
of optimum or workable range is generally considered to be well within the expectation of 
the person skilled in the art.
