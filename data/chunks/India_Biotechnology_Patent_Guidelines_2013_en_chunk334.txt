Chapter 15: Use of inventions for purposes of Government and Acquisition of 
inventions by Central Government 
15.01 Use of inventions for the purposes of Government 
An invention is said to be used for the purpose of Government if it is 
made, used, exercised or vended for the purposes of Central 
Government, a State Government or a Government undertaking.
