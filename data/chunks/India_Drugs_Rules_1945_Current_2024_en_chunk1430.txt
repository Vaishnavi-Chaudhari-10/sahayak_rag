18.5. Packaging operations: 
18.5.1. When the programme for packaging operations is being set up, 
particular attention shall be given to minimising the risk of 
cross-contamination, mix ups or substitutions. Different 
products shall not be packaged in close proximity unless there 
is physical segregation or an alternative system that will 
provide equal assurance.
