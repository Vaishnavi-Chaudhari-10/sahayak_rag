57. Procedure for despatch of sample to Government Analyst. —(1) 
The portion of sample or the container sent by an Inspector to the Government 
Analyst for test or analysis under sub -section (4) of section 23 of the Act shall 
be sent by registered post or by hand in a sealed packet, enclosed together with 
a memorandum in F orm 18, in an outer cover addressed to the Government 
Analyst. 
(2) A copy of the memorandum and a specimen impression of the seal used 
to seal the packet shall be sent to the Government Analyst separately by 
registered post or by hand.
