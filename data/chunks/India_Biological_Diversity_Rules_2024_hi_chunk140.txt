14. Restricting access to biological resources and knowledge associated with such biological resources. –(1)The 
Authority after conducting such enquiry as deemed appropriate in respect of any provisions of this rule, shall take steps 
to restrict or prohibit access to biological resources or knowledge associated with such biological resources for the 
following reasons, namely:- 
(a) the request for access is for any threatened and/ or endemic species including those notified under section 
38 of the Act; 
(b) the request for access may result in an adverse impact on the livelihood and/ or socio-cultural aspects of the 
local communities;
