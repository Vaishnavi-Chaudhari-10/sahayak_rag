10.3. Self-inspection team - Management shall appoint a self -inspection 
team consisting of experts in their respective fields who are familiar 
with GMP. The members of the team may be appointed from inside or 
outside the company. 
10.4. Frequency of self -inspection- The frequency with which self -
inspections are conducted may depend on company requirements but 
shall be at least once in a year. The frequency shall be stated in the 
procedure. 
10.5. Self-inspection report- A report shall be made at the completion of a 
self-inspection. The report shall include the following, namely:- 
(a) self-inspection results; 
(b) evaluation and conclusions; and 
(c) recommended corrective actions. 
10.6. Follow-up action- There shall be an effective follow -up programme.
