possible risks to the subjects, expected benefits and adequacy of documentation 
for ensuring privacy, confidentiality and justice. In the case of any serious 
adverse event occurring to the clinical trial subjects during the clinical trial, the 
Ethics. Committee shall analyse and forward us opinion as per procedures 
specified in APPENDIX XII of Schedule Y.
