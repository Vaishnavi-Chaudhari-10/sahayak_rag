3. Standards— 
(a) Description—A yellowish to brown viscous liquid. 
(b) Identification—It produces hot tense, painful swelling when injected 
into the animals infected with A. mallei organisms. 
(c) Sterility Test—Should comply with the test for sterility described in 
the general monograph on 'Diagnostic Antigens'.
