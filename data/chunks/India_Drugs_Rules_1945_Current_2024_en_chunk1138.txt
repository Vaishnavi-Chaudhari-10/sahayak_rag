3. Proper Name—The proper name of any diagnostic agent is the name 
of microorganism from which it is made, followed by the word 'antigen' unless 
the Schedule otherwise provides, or, it may be derived from the name of the 
organism responsible for the causation of the disease or if there is no special 
provision in the Schedule, the name approved by the Licensing Authority. In the 
case of the undermentioned preparations the proper name of the diagnostic
