SALMONELLA POLLORUM COLOURED ANTIGEN 
1. Synonym—Bacillary White Diarrhoes (B.W.D.) Antigen. 
2. Definition—The antigen is a suspension in a solution containing 1 per 
cent Formaline, 1 per cent KH 2P02 and 0.85 per c ent Sodium Chloride of pure 
smooth culture of a standard strain of Salmonella Pullorum. 
3. Preparation—Standard strain of S. Pullorum is grown on sulphur agar 
medium in Roux flasks for five days at 37°C. The pure growth is washed with 
1.0 per cent Formol Saline. 
Standardisation
