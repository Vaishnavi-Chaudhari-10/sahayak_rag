The ―obviousness‖ must be strictly and objectively judged. While 
determining inventive step, it is important to look at the invention as a 
whole. 
Accordingly, the following points need to be objectively judged to 
ascertain whether, looking at the invention as a whole, the invention 
does have inventive step or not: 
i. Identify the "person skilled in the art", i.e. competent craftsman 
or engineer as distinguished from a mere artisan; 
ii. Identify the relevant common general knowledge of that person 
at the priority date; 
iii. Identify the inventive concept of the claim in question or if that
