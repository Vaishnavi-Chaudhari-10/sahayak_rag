13.3.9. Plant materials, including raw plant materials, shall be kept in 
a dry area protected from moisture and processed following the 
principle of “first in, first out” (FIFO). 
13.4. Production areas- 
13.4.1. Production areas shall comply with the general requirements of 
good manufacturing practices for pharmaceutical products: 
main principles (see Part I). As a rule, campaign work in their 
processing is necessary. However, if feasible, the use of 
dedicated premises is encouraged. Moreover, the special nature 
of the production of Phytopharmaceuticals requires that 
particular attention be given to processing products that 
generate dust. When heating or boiling of the materials is 
necessary, a suitable air exhaust mechanism shall be employed 
to prevent accumulation of fumes and vapours.
