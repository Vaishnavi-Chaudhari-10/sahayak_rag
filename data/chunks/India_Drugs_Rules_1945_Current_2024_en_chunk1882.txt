11.5.1. When an intermediate is intended to be transferred outside the

data derived from stability studies. Common practice is to use 
a retest date, not an expiration date.

procedure that simulates the ﬁnal process to be used on a 
commercial manufacturing scale; and 
(ii) the quality of the API represents the material to be made on 
a commercial scale.
