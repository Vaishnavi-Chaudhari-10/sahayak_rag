Section 123 
17.07 Offences by Companies 
a. When an offence is committed by a company, the company as 
well as every person in charge of, and responsible to the 
company for the conduct of its business at the time of the 
commission of the offence, shall be deemed to be guilty and 
shall be liable to be proceeded against and punished 
accordingly. 
 However, if such person proves that the offence was committed 
without his knowledge or that he exercised all due diligence to 
prevent the commission of the office, he shall not be liable.
