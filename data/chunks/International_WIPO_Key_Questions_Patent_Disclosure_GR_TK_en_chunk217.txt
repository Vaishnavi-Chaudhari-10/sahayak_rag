Box 29: The Nagoya Protocol, 2010
The Nagoya Protocol proposes an international 
framework to ensure that the benefits arising from 
the use of GRs (i.e., “the conduct of research and 
development on the genetic and/or biochemical 
composition of genetic resources”) and associated 
TK are shared with the countries that may 
legitimately provide such resources (i.e., countries 
of origin or other countries that have legally 
acquired GRs).9 Such sharing of benefits shall be
