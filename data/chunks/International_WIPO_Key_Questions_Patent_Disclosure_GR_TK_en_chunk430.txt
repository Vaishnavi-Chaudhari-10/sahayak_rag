Article 120-A.- Non-compliance with the 
rules applicable to genetic resources and 
traditional knowledge
Non-compliance by the patent applicant with the 
contract requirement stated in Article 26(h) and 
(i) of Andean Community Commission Decision 
No. 486 and developed in Articles 20 and 21 of 
the Regulation on Access to Genetic Resources 
shall be punishable by one or more of the following 
penalties, unless the applicant withdraws from 
the patent grant procedure or satisfactorily ex-
plains that the invention does not use traditional 
knowledge or genetic resource originating in Peru:
(a) a fine of up to 1,000 UIT;
(b) compensation;
(c) fair and equitable sharing of benefits, 
including payment of royalties and/or other 
monetary or non-monetary measures;
(d) technology transfer and capacity building;
(e) use authorizations.
