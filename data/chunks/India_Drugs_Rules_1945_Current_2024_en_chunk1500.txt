6.8. There shall be a clear means of differentiating products that have not 
been sterilised from those which have. Each basket, tray, or other carrier 
of products or components shall be clearly labelled with the name of the 
material, its batch number and an indication of whether or not it has 
been sterilised. Indicators such as autoclave tape may be used where 
appropriate to indicate whether or not a batch (or sub -batch) has passed 
through a sterilisation process, but they do not give a reliable indication 
that the batch is in fact sterile. 
6.9. Validated loading patterns shall be established for all sterilisation 
processes.
