Chapter 2
Section 8b.
If an invention concerns or uses biological material 
or traditional knowledge, the patent application 
shall include information on the country from 
which the inventor collected or received the 
material or the knowledge (the providing 
country). If it follows from the national law in 
the providing country that access to biological 
material or use of traditional knowledge shall be 
subject to prior consent, the application shall 
state whether such consent has been obtained.
