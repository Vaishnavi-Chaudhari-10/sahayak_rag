Guiding 
Principle 1 
If the claims are for extracts/alkaloids and/or isolated active ingredients 
of plants, which are naturally/inherently present in plants, such claims 
cannot be considered as novel and/or inventive when use of such plants 
is known in prior art. However, processes for obtaining such 
extracts/isolates may be considered patentable, subject to the 
requirements of patentability.
