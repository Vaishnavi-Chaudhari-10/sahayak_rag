14.5. Returns- 
14.5.1. Returned intermediates or APIs shall be identiﬁed as such and 
quarantined. 
14.5.2. If the conditions under which returned intermediates or APIs 
have been stored or shipped before or during their return or the 
condition of their containers casts doubt on their quality, the 
returned intermediates or APIs shall be reprocessed, reworked 
or destroyed, as appropriate. 
14.5.3. Records of returned intermediates or APIs shall be maintained. 
For each return, documentation shall include- 
(i) name and address of the consignee; 
(ii) intermediate or API, batch number and quantity returned; 
(iii) reasons for return; and 
(iv) use or disposal of the returned intermediate or API.
