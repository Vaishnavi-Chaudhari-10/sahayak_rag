1.26. Material Pass -Through-Hatches (PTH) or Pass Boxes (PB) can also be 
used for separating two different zones. PTHs fall into two categories, 
namely a dynamic PTH or a passive PTH. Dynamic PTHs have an air 
supply to or extraction from them, and can then be us ed as bubble, sink 
or cascade PTHs. 
1.27. Where appropriate, temperature and relative humidity shall be controlled, 
monitored and recorded, where relevant, to ensure compliance with 
requirements pertinent to the materials and products and provide a 
comfortable environment for the operator where necessary. 
1.28. Maximum and minimum room temperatures and relative humidity shall 
be appropriate. Alert and action limits on temperatures and humidity 
shall be set, as appropriate.
