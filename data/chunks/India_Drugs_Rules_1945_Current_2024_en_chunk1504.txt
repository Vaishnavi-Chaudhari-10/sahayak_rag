6.11.1.5. The items to be sterilised, other than products in sealed 
containers, shall be wrapped in a material that allows the 
removal of air and the penetration of steam but prevents 
recontamination afte r sterilisation. Specially designed 
autoclavable stainless steel containers, that allow steam to 
enter and air to leave, can also be used. All parts of the load 
shall be in contact with water or saturated steam at the 
required temperature for the required time. 
6.11.1.6. Care shall be taken to ensure that the steam used for 
sterilisation is of suitable quality chemical, microbiological 
and endotoxin analysis of condensate and physical 
examination of steam (such as dryness, superheat and non -
condensable gases) and doe s not contain additives at a level 
that could cause contamination of the product or equipment. 
Steam used for sterilisation shall be tested regularly. 
6.11.1.7. Sterilisation by dry heat may be suitable for non -aqueous 
liquids or dry-powde
