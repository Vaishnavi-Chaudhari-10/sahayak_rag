International Bureau as Receiving Office 
An international patent application can be directly filed with IB along 
with the prescribed fee, in request form (PCT/RO/ 101), in 
duplicate. Permission u/s 39 is required for filing directly in IB. Such 
application can also be filed electronically via e-PCT route.
