Article 173. Observations
7. Before rejecting in full or in part an application 
or a related request, for reasons that were not set 
forth in observations pursuant to paragraph 1, 
the Italian Patent and Trademark Office assigns 
the applicant a term of two months to submit 
observations. Once that term has expired, if 
no observations have been submitted or if the 
Office does not believe that it can accept those 
submitted, the application or request is rejected 
in full or in part.
