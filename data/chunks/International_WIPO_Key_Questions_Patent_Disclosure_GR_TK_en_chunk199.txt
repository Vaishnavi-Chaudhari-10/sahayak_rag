The National Biodiversity Authority is the 
national competent authority for the decisions 
related to ABS including PIC and prior 
approval for applying for IP based on GRs and 
TK obtained from India. All IP applications 
in regard to biological resources require the 
express permission of the National Biodiversity 
Authority before the patent application is filed.
National Biodiversity Authority approval ensures 
that the terms and conditions subject to which 
approval is granted secures equitable sharing 
of benefits arising out of the use of accessed 
biological resources, their byproducts, innovations 
and practices associated with their use and 
applications and knowledge relating thereto in 
accordance with MAT and conditions between 
the person applying for such approval, local 
bodies concerned and the benefit claimers.
