Take tender fruit of Agastya. Cut the fruits into finger like pieces (1 – 
2 inches). Take in a stainless steel vessel ,add required quantity of 
water and lavana and cook until the fruit becomes soft. Once properly 
cooked, add milk to the vessel. Then add a paste of Tila and Narikela, 
Kshiravataka (a milk based preparation), Kushmanda vataka (fried in
