7. Personnel:- 
7.1. Only the minimum number of personnel required shall be present in 
clean areas; this is particularly important during aseptic processes. As 
far as possible, inspections and controls shall be conducted from 
outside such areas. 
7.2. All personnel (including those concerned with cleaning and 
maintenance) employed in such areas shall receive initial and regular 
training in disciplines relevant to the correct manufactu re of sterile 
products, including hygiene and the basic elements of microbiology.
