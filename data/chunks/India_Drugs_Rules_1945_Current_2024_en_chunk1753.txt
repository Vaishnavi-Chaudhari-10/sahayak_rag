1.22. Central dust extraction systems shall be interlocked with the appropriate 
air-handling systems, to ensure that they operate simultaneously. 
1.23. Room pressure differential between adjacent cubicles, which are linked 
by common dust extraction ducting, shall be avoided. 
1.24. Air shall not flow through the dust extraction ducting or return air 
ducting from the room w ith the higher pressure to the room with the 
lower pressure (this would normally occur only if extract or return 
systems were inoperative). Systems shall be designed to prevent dust 
flowing back in the opposite direction in the event of component failure 
or airflow failure.
