Brief on AYUSH-64
The CCRAS, an apex body for R&D in Ayurveda under the Ministry of Ayush, Govt. of
India developed a poly-herbal anti-malarial drug ‘AYUSH-64’ in 1980 through extensive
pharmacological, toxicological and clinical studies. It has been patented by the Central
Council for Research in Ayurvedic Sciences CCRAS through National Research and
Development Corporation, New Delhi. Its efficacy and safety has already been proven in
infective febrile conditions such as malaria, microfilaremia, chikungunya, and flu-like
illness. It was repurposed for COVID-19 based on the recommendations of Interdisciplinary
Ayush R & D Task Force. A molecular docking study conducted at ICMR-NIN,
Hyderabad that revealed 35 phytoconstituents isolated from AYUSH-64 demonstrated anti-
viral activity against SARS-CoV-2.
