6.3. The microbial contamination of starting materials shall be minimal and 
their bio burden s hall be monitored before sterilisation. Specifications 
shall include requirements for microbiological quality when the need for 
this has been indicated by monitoring. 
6.4. All sterilisation processes shall be validated. Particular attention shall be 
paid when the adopted sterilisation method is used for a preparation that 
is not a simple aqueous or oily solution, for example, colloidal 
suspensions.
