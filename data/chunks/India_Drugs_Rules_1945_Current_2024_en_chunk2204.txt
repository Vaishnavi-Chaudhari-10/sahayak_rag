1.7 Carcinogenicity (See Appendix 1, Item 4.8) 
Carcinogenicity studies should be performed for all drugs that are expected to 
be clinically used for more than 6 months as well as for drugs used frequently in 
an intermittent manner in the treatment of chronic or recurrent conditions.
