9. Storage—Bacterial vaccines shall be stored, protected from light at

ANTHRAX SPORE VACCINE (LIVING) 
1. Synonyms—A virulent Anthrax Spore Vaccine or Bacillus Anthracis 
Vaccine (Living). 
2. Definition—The vaccine is a suspension of living spores of an 
uncapsulated avirulent strain of B. anthracis in 50 per cent glycerine saline.
