(a) all personnel shall undergo medical examination prior to 
employment and shall be free from infectious and contagious diseases 
and thereafter they should be medically examined periodically at least 
once a year and for this purpose records shall be maintained thereof; 
(b) all personnel, prior to and during employment, shall be trained in
