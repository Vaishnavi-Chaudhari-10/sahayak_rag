Rule 19M 
08.16 Conditions for and extent of refund 
The fee paid by the applicant may be refunded, waived or reduced to 
the extent and in accordance with the conditions specified in the Treaty 
or the regulations under the Treaty and the agreement entered between 
the Indian Patent Office and the International Bureau.
