(b) Identification—All types of vaccines confer active immunity in the 
susceptible animals which can be demonstrated by injecting suitable 
experimental animals with th e calculated doses of the product and 
subsequently determining the presence of the protective antibodies in 
their serum and/or by challenging the vaccinated animals by injecting 
virulent strain of the homologous organisms. The protected animals 
should survive the challenge.
