6.10. Sterilisation records shall be available for each sterilisation run. They 
shall be approved as part of the batch-release procedure. 
6.11.1. Terminal sterilisation- 
6.11.1.1. Each heat -sterilisation cycle shall be recorded by means of 
appropriate equipment of suitable accuracy and precision, e.g., 
on a time or temperature chart with a suitably large scale. The 
temperature shall be recorded by a probe situated at the 
coolest part of the load or loaded chamber, this point having 
been determined during the validation; the temperature shall 
preferably be checked against a second independent 
temperature probe located at the same position. Sterilisation 
records shall be available for each sterilisation run and shall be 
approved as part of the batch release procedure. Chemical or 
biological indicators may also be used but shall not take the 
place of physical controls.
