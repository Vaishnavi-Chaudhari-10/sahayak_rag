5. Environmental protection:- 
5.1. Due to the hazardous nature of the products being handled in the facility, 
neither the product nor its residues shall be allowed to escape into the 
atmosphere or to be discharged directly to normal drainage systems. 
5.2. The external atmosphere and the public in the vicinity of the facility shall 
be protected from possible harm from hazardous substances.
