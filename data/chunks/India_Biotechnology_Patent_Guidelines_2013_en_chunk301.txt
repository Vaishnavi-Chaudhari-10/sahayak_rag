12.01.02 Appeals 
a) No appeal to IPAB shall lie from any order or direction of the 
Central Government, or of the Controller, except in case of 
orders/decisions, issued by the Controller or Central Government 
under Sections 15, 16,17, 18, 19, 20, 25(4), 28, 51, 54, 57, 60, 61, 
63, 66, 69(3), 78,84(1) to (5), 85, 88, 91, 92 and 94 of the Act. 
b) No appeal lies from an Order of the Controller granting 
extension of time when such extension is provided in any provision 
of the Act or of the rules made thereunder.
