Under “additional provisions applicable to applications 
for registration of inventions concerning gene source

5. Legal nature

21
Legal nature
“[A]n application for registration of an invention 
concerning gene source or traditional knowledge 
must also contain documents explaining the origin 
of the gene source and/or traditional knowledge 
accessed by the inventor or the applicant, if the in-
vention is directly based on that gene source and/or 
traditional knowledge. If the inventor or the applicant 
cannot identify the origin of the gene source and/
or traditional knowledge, he/she shall so declare 
and bear responsibility for the truthfulness of his/
her declaration.”
