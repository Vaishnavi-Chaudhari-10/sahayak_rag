14.4. Recovery of materials and solvents- 
14.4.1. Recovery (e.g., from mother liquor or ﬁltrates) of reactants, 
intermediates or the API is considered acceptable, provided that 
approved procedures exist for the recovery and the recovered 
materials meet speciﬁcations suitable for their intended use. 
14.4.2. Solvents can be recovered and reused in the same processes or 
in different processes, provided that the recovery procedures 
are controlled and monitored to ensure that solvents meet 
appropriate standards before reuse or comingling with other 
approved materials. 
14.4.3. Fresh and recovered solvents and reagents can be combined, if 
adequate testing has shown their suitability for all 
manufacturing processes in which they may be used. 
14.4.4. The use of recovered solvents, mother liquors and other 
recovered materials shall be adequately documented.
