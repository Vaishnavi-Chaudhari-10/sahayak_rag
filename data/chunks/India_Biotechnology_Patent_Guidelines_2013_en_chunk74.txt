Chapter - 5: Provisional and Complete Specification 
05.01 Specification 
 The Specification is a techno -legal document containing scientific and 
technical disclosure and claims for the invention which is the basis of 
rights of a patent. The Specification, thus, forms a crucial part of the 
patent application. 
A specification may be filed either as a p rovisional or complete 
specification. 
 Provisional or Complete Specification shall be submitted in Form -2 
along with the Application Form -1 and other documents 
accompanied with the prescribed fee as given in the First Schedule.
