5. Tests—Viral vaccine shall be tested for sterility, safety and potency on 
suitable test animals and for viability in the case of live vaccines. 
(a) Sterility Test—All Vaccines shall be tested for sterility in accordance 
with Rules 115 to 119. If the vaccine contains ad ded bactericides or 
bacteriostatic, a quantity of a medium sufficient to render the growth 
inhibitor ineffective is added to the sample or a suitable substance is added 
in a concentration sufficient to render the growth inhibitor ineffective but 
not itself to inhibit the growth of micro-organisms.
