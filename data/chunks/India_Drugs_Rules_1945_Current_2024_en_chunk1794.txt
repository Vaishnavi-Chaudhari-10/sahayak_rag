1.2. Scope- 
1.2.1. This Part applies to the manufacture of APIs for use in Finished 
Pharmaceutical Products (FPPs). It applies to the manufacture of 
sterile APIs only up to the point immediat ely prior to the APIs 
being rendered sterile. The sterilisation and aseptic processing of 
sterile APIs are not covered by this Part, but shall be performed in 
accordance with GMP guidelines for sterile products.
