4. Standards— 
(a) Description—Violet coloured liquid containing dead bacteria in 
suspension. 
(b) Identification—It gives specific agglutination when mixed with the 
serum of birds infected with S. Pullorum infection. It is used for carrying 
out plate agglutination test for serological diagnosis for S. Pullorum 
infection in birds.
