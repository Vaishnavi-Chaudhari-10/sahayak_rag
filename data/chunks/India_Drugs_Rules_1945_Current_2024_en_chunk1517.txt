6.11.3.4. Isolators shall be introduced only after appropriate 
validation. Validation shall take into account all critical 
factors of isolator technology, for example, the quality of the 
air inside and outside (back ground) the isolator, sanitisation 
of the isolator, the transfer process and isolator integrity. 
6.11.3.5. Monitoring shall be done routinely and shall include 
frequent leak testing of the isolator and the glove or sleeve 
system.
