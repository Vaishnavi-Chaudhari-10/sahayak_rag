(d) Purity Tests for Living Bacterial Vaccine —Petri-dishes containing 
suitable media are streaked with the final product and incubated at 37°C 
for 72 hours. The vaccine passes the test if no growth of micro -organisms 
other than those from which the vaccine was prepared is observed. Other 
tests include examination for motility of the organisms, fermentation 
reactions and thermoagglutination test and dye -inhibitor test in case of 
bruceliza vaccine. 
(e) Safety Test—The safety of the vaccine shall be assessed by injecting 
it in appropriate dose in suitable susceptible animals. No animal should 
show any untoward, general or local reaction, within seven days after 
inoculation.
